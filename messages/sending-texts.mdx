Send a text from an existing conversation or a contact's **Messages** tab. Your agency needs an active texting number provisioned by PolicyDen support.

## Before you start

- Check the contact's phone number.
- Keep the text within 500 characters.
- Attach no more than 10 files, totaling no more than 5 MB.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    In **Messages**, select the contact’s conversation. For a new conversation, open the contact profile’s **Messages** tab.

<Frame caption="Conversation with a fictional client, message history, and the Send Message field.">
  <video className="w-full rounded-xl motion-reduce:hidden" autoPlay muted loop playsInline controls preload="metadata" poster="/images/messages/send-conversation.png" src="/videos/messages/send-conversation.mp4" aria-label="Conversation with a fictional client, message history, and the Send Message field." />
  <img className="hidden motion-reduce:block" src="/images/messages/send-conversation.png" alt="Conversation with a fictional client, message history, and the Send Message field." />
</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your message">
    In **Send Message...**, enter the text you want to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add optional attachments">
    Select the paperclip to attach files, if needed. You can send files with or without text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the message">
    Select **Send**. The message appears in the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When available, the contact-card control offers **Attach Agency vCard**, **Attach Agent vCard**, or **Attach Business Card**. These add contact details or a business-card image.

The agency option appears when your agency has a vCard set up. Agent cards and business-card images come from the agent's profile.

## What you'll see

Your outgoing message appears in the thread with its sending status; appearing there does not by itself confirm delivery.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Send is disabled">
    Enter text or attach a file. Selecting only a contact card does not enable **Send**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message failed">
    Open [My text didn’t send](/fix/text-didnt-send) for the exact error and next action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The conversation will not load">
    For **Failed to load messages**, reopen the conversation and check your connection.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
