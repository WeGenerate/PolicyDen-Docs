Messages is PolicyDen's SMS inbox. Every text you send to a contact and every reply they send back lives on their contact record, so the whole team can see the conversation history without digging through personal phones.

## Before you start

Your agency needs at least one active SMS number configured. Admins set that up in [SMS numbers](/settings/sms-numbers). Once a number is active, texting works from the main **Messages** page, from a contact's profile, or from the conversation itself.

## The conversation list

Open **Messages** from the sidebar. The left column lists every contact who has texted with your agency, most recent first.

Each row shows:

- The contact's name and last message preview.
- A timestamp — time today, **Yesterday**, weekday for the past week, or a date beyond that.
- A red badge with the unread count, when they have unread incoming messages.

Use the filter dropdown at the top of the list to switch between:

| Filter | What it shows |
| ------ | ------------- |
| **All Messages** | Every conversation, active first. |
| **Unread** | Conversations with messages you haven't opened yet. |
| **Read** | Conversations where nothing is waiting on you. |
| **Archived** | Conversations you've set aside. |

Sort toggles between **Recent First** and **Oldest First**. The search box filters by contact name, phone number, or the text inside any message.

<Tip>
  If you work a large book, save time by switching to **Unread** first thing in the morning — you'll see only the conversations that need a response.
</Tip>

## Send a message

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a conversation">
    Click a contact in the list. If the contact has never been texted, start from their profile — the **SMS** tab on the [contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile) opens the same conversation view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your message">
    Enter your text in the composer at the bottom. Press **Enter** to send, **Shift + Enter** for a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach files (optional)">
    Click the paperclip to attach images, PDFs, audio, video, or vCards. You can attach up to 10 files per message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send**. The message appears in the thread with a delivery indicator:
    - Green checkmark: delivered.
    - Amber alert: the carrier reported undelivered.
    - Red X: the send failed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If a contact has unsubscribed from SMS, the composer is replaced with a notice — you can't text them until they opt back in.
</Note>

## The conversation view

Inside a conversation you'll see:

- **Conversation thread** on the left, grouped by day with **Today**, **Yesterday**, and dated headers.
- **Contact details** on the right — phone, email, date of birth, pipeline stage, address, and any linked policies.
- A **Call** button in the header that starts an outbound call to the contact through the [in-app dialer](/calls/overview).
- A **View Profile** button that opens the full [contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile) in a new tab.

Messages sent by teammates show the sender's name and avatar above the bubble.

## Mark conversations as read

Opening a conversation automatically marks every incoming message in it as read. The red badge clears, and the global unread counter in the sidebar updates.

To clear every unread message at once:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Messages">
    Go to **Messages** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply any filter you want to clear">
    For example, stay on **All Messages** to clear everything, or switch to **Unread** to scope the action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Mark all as read">
    The button sits at the bottom of the conversation list. It appears when unread messages exist in the current view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Archive a conversation

Archive conversations you're done with so your main inbox stays focused. Archived conversations don't show in **All Messages** — switch the filter to **Archived** to see them.

Unarchiving a conversation is the reverse: open it from the **Archived** filter and send a new message, or restore it to return it to your main list.

<Note>
  If you can't find the archive or unarchive action on a conversation, contact [support](mailto:support@policyden.com) — the entry point varies by plan.
</Note>
