## Who sees what

Agents see conversations for their assigned contacts. Admins and Agency Owners can review agency conversations and filter by **Agent**.

Open **Messages** for the full inbox, or use the floating message button to read and reply while keeping your current page open. A contact's **Messages** tab also shows their text history.

Texting requires an active agency texting number. PolicyDen support provisions these numbers; contact support if your agency needs one.

The inbox shows contacts with message history. To start a conversation with another contact, open that contact's **Messages** tab.
