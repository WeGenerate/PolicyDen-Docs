<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners.</Info>

Ask about a contact, policy, or reporting period. Include the dates and details you want Atlas to use.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Atlas">
  In the sidebar, select **Atlas**.
</Step>
<Step title="Enter your question">
  In **Ask Atlas a question**, enter a specific question, such as “What policies go effective this week?”
</Step>
<Step title="Send the question">
  Select **Send message**, or press Enter. Use Shift+Enter for a new line.
</Step>
<Step title="Check the answer">
  Review the answer and open any relevant record links to confirm the details.
</Step>
<Step title="Refine your question">
  In **Ask a follow-up question**, add a date range, correction, or more specific request.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/atlas/ask-atlas-prompt.png" alt="Atlas question field containing an example question before sending." />
</Frame>

## What you'll see

Atlas shows activity while preparing a reply, followed by an answer that may include record links or a sortable table.

Use **Stop generating** to stop an answer in progress. If a result includes a table, use **Export CSV** to download it. Select **New chat** when you want a separate conversation.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I can't send my question">
  Enter nonblank text and keep it within the 2,000-character limit. Wait for the current response or stop it before sending another question.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="An earlier chat won't load">
  Refresh Atlas and reopen the conversation. If **Could not load messages** remains, contact support@policyden.com.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The answer doesn't match my records">
  Check your dates and record access. Ask a more specific follow-up and verify the answer against the contact or policy.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
