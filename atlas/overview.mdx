Atlas is the AI assistant built into PolicyDen. Ask it a question in plain English and it answers using the contacts, policies, messages, and activity in your agency. Use it to look things up, summarize your pipeline, or pull a quick stat without building a report.

</Frame>

## Open Atlas

Click **Atlas** in the sidebar, or open it from the **Add new** menu on any page. The first time you visit, you'll land on a blank canvas with a prompt — *What can I help you with?* — and a few starter suggestions.

Suggested prompts cover the kinds of things Atlas is good at:

- "How are my agents performing this month?"
- "What policies go effective this week?"
- "Show me our carrier breakdown."
- "Find contacts named Smith."

Click any suggestion to run it as-is, or type your own question.

## Ask a question

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type your question">
    Enter anything in the composer. Press **Enter** to send, or **Shift + Enter** for a new line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the answer">
    Atlas shows a thinking indicator while it works, then streams the response into the thread. You'll see your question on the right and the answer on the left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask follow-ups">
    Keep typing in the composer at the bottom. Atlas remembers the conversation, so you can refine or drill in: *"Just the ones in California"*, *"Sort by effective date"*, *"Summarize that as bullet points"*.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Atlas works best with specific, concrete questions. *"Who are my top five agents by policies written this quarter?"* gets a better answer than *"How's the team doing?"*
</Tip>

## Actions on each answer

Hover any answer from Atlas to reveal two buttons:

- **Copy** — copies the response to your clipboard.
- **Try again** — regenerates the last answer. Atlas retracts your previous question and answer, then re-runs your question to produce a fresh response.

<Note>
  Atlas can make mistakes. Double-check any number, name, or date it gives you before acting on it — especially for quotes, renewals, or anything you'd send to a client.
</Note>

## Conversation history

Every exchange with Atlas is saved as a conversation in the sidebar on the left, grouped by when you last used it:

- **Today**
- **Yesterday**
- **Previous 7 days**
- **Previous 30 days**
- **Older**

The most recent conversations load by default. Click **View more** to see older ones. Click any conversation to reopen it — Atlas picks up where you left off.

### Start a new conversation

Click **New chat** at the top of the sidebar. You'll get a fresh canvas without context from earlier conversations. Use a new chat when you switch topics — it gives Atlas a cleaner slate and usually faster answers.

### Delete a conversation

Hover a conversation in the sidebar and click the trash icon. Confirm the prompt. The conversation and every message in it are permanently deleted.

## Rate limits

Atlas has a short rate limit to keep things responsive for everyone in your agency. If you send too many questions too quickly, you'll see a brief "try again in a moment" message. Wait a few seconds and send again.
