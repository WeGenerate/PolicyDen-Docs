## Who sees what

Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners can open **Atlas** from the sidebar. Answers use the data your account can access.

Select a suggested question to send it immediately, or write your own. Individual agent accounts get suggestions about personal performance; agency accounts get team-oriented suggestions.

The conversation list lets you reopen earlier chats or select **New chat**. On smaller screens, use **Open conversations** to reach it.

Atlas can make mistakes. Check names, dates, totals, and linked records before using an answer for client work.
