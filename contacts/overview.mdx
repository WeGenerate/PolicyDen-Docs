Contacts brings your leads and clients into one list. Open a contact to review their policies, messages, notes, and follow-up work.

Use **Search** to find a record, or narrow the list with filters. Select a contact’s name to open their profile. Scroll through the table to load more results.

## Who sees what

Agents start with **My Contacts** turned on. Clear it to see other contacts available within your agency access. Agents whose work location is set to Remote can only see their assigned contacts; they don’t see this checkbox.

Admins and Agency Owners can import contacts and select rows for [bulk changes](/contacts/bulk-actions). Everyone can create contacts and export records they can access.

Individual agent accounts don’t show **Agent** or **Agency** filters or columns.
