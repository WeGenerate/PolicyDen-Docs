A contact's profile is where you do the day-to-day work for that person — review their policies, schedule follow-ups, send a message, drop a note for the team, and see everything that's ever been done on the record.

## Open a profile

Click a contact's name anywhere it appears — in the contact list, in search, on a policy, or on a task.

## Layout

Every contact profile uses the same two-column layout.

- **Header** — avatar, name, and quick actions.
- **Left column** — tabbed workspace for policies, tasks, documents, notes, comments, history, and messages.
- **Right column** — contact details, doctors, and medications, each in a collapsible section.

## Header

The top of the profile shows the contact's avatar and full name alongside a row of quick-action buttons.

| Button | What it does |
| ------ | ------------ |
| **Add Policy** | Opens the Add Policy modal pre-scoped to this contact. |
| **Tasks** | Creates a task for this contact. |
| **Schedule** | Opens the meeting scheduler. |
| **Add Note** | Writes a note attached to this contact. |
| **Attachments** | Uploads a document to the contact. |

## Right column

Three sections you can expand or collapse independently.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Contact Details" icon="user">
    First name, last name, email, phone, date of birth, gender, physical address, mailing address, Medicare number, and any custom fields your agency has defined. Every field is editable in place — click a value, change it, and save.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Doctors" icon="stethoscope">

  </Accordion>

  </Accordion>

## Tabs

The tabs on the left side of the profile each focus on one kind of record.

### Policies

Every policy you've logged against the contact, newest first. Click **Add Policy** in the header to add a new one. Click a policy card to open the [policy profile](/policies/policy-profile).

### Tasks

Every task tied to the contact. You see the task's priority, due date, status, and assignee. Open the [Tasks overview](/tasks/overview) for the full rundown.

### Docs

Files uploaded to the contact — enrollment forms, scope of appointment, signed paperwork, anything else. Drag and drop a file into the upload zone to add it. See [Documents](/customization/documents) for allowed file types and limits.

### Notes

Longer, structured notes about the contact. Each note has a title and a body and is attributed to whoever wrote it. Use notes for client summaries and context you want to find again later.

### Comments

A thread of short comments, newest at the top. Use comments to loop teammates in on a specific situation. See [Notes and comments](/customization/notes-and-comments) for how notes and comments differ.

### History

A chronological log of every change made to the contact — who created it, who updated which fields, when it was reassigned. Useful for auditing and for seeing what a teammate did without having to ask.

### Messages

Both SMS and email conversations with the contact live here. Use the dropdown at the top of the tab to switch between:

- **SMS** — every text to and from the contact, including attachments.
- **Emails** — every email to and from the contact.

You can send a new SMS directly from this tab, as long as the contact is subscribed and your agency has an active SMS number. See [Messages overview](/messages/overview) for the full SMS experience and [SMS numbers](/settings/sms-numbers) to set one up.
