Open a name in **Contacts** to see that contact’s profile. It brings their policies, communication, files, and follow-up work into one place.

<Frame caption="A contact profile with policies and contact details.">
  <img src="/images/contacts/profile-overview.png" alt="Full contact profile showing its tabs, action buttons, and Contact Details panel." />
</Frame>

Use **Messages** to review communication. **Policies** shows coverage records; **Documents** holds files; **Notes** holds written context; **History** shows recorded changes. Follow-up tasks and meetings are available from the profile.

The **Contact Details** panel contains editable contact information. Expand its sections to review addresses and additional fields. **Missing Required Information** lists details that still need attention.

## Who sees what

Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners can open profiles they have access to. Agents whose work location is set to Remote can only open contacts assigned to them.

**Call** appears when the dialer is enabled and the contact has a phone number. Admins and Agency Owners also see the editable **Agent** field in **Contact Details**.
