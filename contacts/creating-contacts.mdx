Use **Add Contact** to create a new lead or client. The flow checks for duplicates before you fill in anything else, so you don't end up with two records for the same person.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/add-contact-modal.png" alt="Add New Contact modal prompting you to search for an existing contact by phone number before creating a new record" />
</Frame>

## Open the Add Contact modal

You can start a new contact from a few places:

- The **Add Contact** button on the [Contacts page](/contacts/overview).
- The **Add new** menu in the top bar.
- The plus icon next to the **Contact** column header in the contact list.

## Step 1: Search by phone

The first screen asks for a phone number. This is the number you spoke to the client on — it's what PolicyDen uses to check for an existing contact.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the phone number">
    Type the phone number into the **Phone** field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the search">
    Click **Search** or press Enter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

PolicyDen compares the number against every contact in your agency. Secondary phone numbers are checked too.

## Step 2: Pick an existing contact or create a new one

What happens next depends on what the search returned.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="A match was found">
    If PolicyDen finds one or more contacts with that phone, you'll see them in a list. Each row shows the contact's name and the status of their most recent policy.

    Click any result to open that contact's profile instead of creating a new record.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="No match was found">
    If the phone number isn't in your agency, PolicyDen opens the create form. The **Phone** field is locked to what you searched for, so you don't have to retype it.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 3: Fill in the required details

The create form asks for the minimum PolicyDen needs to start tracking the contact. Every field below is required.

| Field | Notes |
| ----- | ----- |
| **Phone** | Carried over from the search step. Locked once you reach the create form. |
| **First Name** | Contact's legal first name. |
| **Last Name** | Contact's legal last name. |
| **Medicare Number** | The contact's Medicare Beneficiary Identifier (MBI). |
| **Date of Birth** | Format: MM/DD/YYYY. You can type the date or pick it from the calendar. |

Click **Add Contact** to save.

## After you save

PolicyDen does three things:

1. Creates the contact with a status of **Lead** and assigns them to you.
2. Shows a confirmation toast.
3. Opens the new contact's [profile](/contacts/contact-profile) so you can add more details or log a policy.

From the profile you can fill in email, address, gender, custom fields, and more — all of it is optional at creation time and can be added later.

## Duplicate detection

PolicyDen checks for duplicates whenever you start the Add Contact flow:

- **By phone** — the primary phone and secondary phone on every contact in your agency are searched. The phone must be at least 10 digits to trigger a check.
- **By Medicare number** — if you search by MBI instead of (or in addition to) phone, PolicyDen compares the uppercased MBI (minimum 11 characters).

If PolicyDen finds a match, it shows you the existing contact before you can create a new one. That prevents two agents from creating duplicate records for the same client.

<Warning>
  Only agencies you belong to are searched. If a contact lives in another agency on the same PolicyDen tenant, it won't show up.
</Warning>
