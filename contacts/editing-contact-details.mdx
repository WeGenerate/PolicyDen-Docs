Update contact information directly in the profile’s **Contact Details** panel. Confirm each field separately to save your changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact profile">
    In **Contacts**, select the contact’s name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand contact details">
    Open **Contact Details** if the panel is collapsed.

    <Frame caption="Find editable information in Contact Details.">
      <img src="/images/contacts/editing-details.png" alt="Contact profile with the Contact Details panel highlighted." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the field">
    Select the current value beside **Phone**, **Email**, or another field you want to change.

    Expand **Physical Address** or **Mailing Address** to edit address fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the new value">
    Replace the current value with the correct information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the change">
    Select the checkmark beside the edited field to save it.

    For text fields, you can also press Enter. Select the cross to cancel a text change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Admins and Agency Owners can change the assigned **Agent** from this panel.</Note>

## What you'll see

A **Contact updated** message confirms the save, and the profile displays the new value.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The change wasn’t saved">
    Confirm the edited field with its checkmark. Moving away from a text field doesn’t save a changed value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Update failed appears">
    Read the validation message, correct the value, and confirm it again. If the request failed, retry when your connection returns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Required information is still missing">
    Review **Missing Required Information** and complete the fields it lists. Your agency can require additional fields.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
