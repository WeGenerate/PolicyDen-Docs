Bulk actions let Admins act on many contacts in a single step — reassign a batch to a different Agent, or archive a list you no longer need in view.

<Info>
  Bulk actions are Admin-only. Agents don't see the selection checkboxes or the bulk action bar.
</Info>

## Select contacts

Open the [Contacts page](/contacts/overview). A checkbox column appears on the left of the table.

- Click the checkbox on a row to select that contact.
- Click the checkbox in the table header to select every contact on screen.
- Use the search, filters, and date range to narrow the list first, then select what's left.

The **bulk action bar** slides up from the bottom of the screen as soon as one contact is selected. It shows how many are selected and the actions available.

## Assign agent

Reassign the selected contacts to a different user in your agency.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Assign To dialog">
    In the bulk action bar, click **Assign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new Agent">
    Select an Agent from the dropdown. The list includes every user in your agency.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Assign**. Every selected contact is reassigned to that Agent, and the Agent column updates in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Only the **Agent** column changes. The contact's agency, status, policies, tasks, and history all stay the same.
</Tip>

## Archive contacts

Archive selected contacts to remove them from the list without permanently deleting them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the archive dialog">
    In the bulk action bar, click **Archive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the confirmation text and click **Archive Contacts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Archived contacts disappear from the contact list, pipeline, reports, and other places they normally appear. Their policies, tasks, and messages stay on record, and an archive can be reversed later.
</Warning>

Use archive when you want to clean up leads that are no longer in play — not as a way to correct a duplicate. For one-off mistakes, archive from the individual contact's profile instead.
