description: "Select contacts and change their assignment or remove them from lists."

<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners, including owners of individual agent accounts.</Info>

Use the selection bar to reassign or archive several contacts together. Start with a filtered list to narrow your selection.

## Before you start

- Review the contacts you plan to change.
- For reassignment, know which agency user should receive the contacts.

## Select your contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your contact list">
    Go to **Contacts** and apply the filters you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the rows">
    Select each contact's checkbox. The header checkbox selects all loaded rows, including rows outside the visible area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the selection count">
    Review the **Selected** count in the action bar before continuing.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/contacts/bulk-selection.png" alt="Contacts list with the selection bar highlighted." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reassign selected contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the assignment dialog">
    In the selection bar, select **Assign To**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an agency user">
    In **Select agent**, choose the user who should receive the contacts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
    Select **Assign**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Admins can also change one contact's **Agent** field on its profile.

## Archive selected contacts

<Warning>Archiving removes contacts from lists. There is no restore control in the app; review your selection before confirming.</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the archive dialog">
    In the selection bar, select **Archive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the confirmation">
    Check that you intend to remove every selected contact. Select **Cancel** to keep them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the archive">
    Select **Archive Contacts**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Reassigned contacts show their new agent; archived contacts disappear from the list.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see selection checkboxes">
    Bulk selection is available to Admins and Agency Owners. Ask your agency administrator to check your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Assign is disabled">
    Choose a user in **Select agent** before confirming.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No contacts appear">
    Adjust your filters, then review the resulting list before selecting rows.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
