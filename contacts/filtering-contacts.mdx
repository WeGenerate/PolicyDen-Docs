Find the contacts you need with search, filters, and sorting. You can only find records available within your agency access.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact list">
    Go to **Contacts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for a contact">
    In **Search**, enter part of the contact’s name, email, phone number, or Medicare number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your filters">
    Open **Status**, **Gender**, or **State**, then select the values to include.

    Use **Exclude** to leave out selected values. Agency accounts also show **Agency** and **Agent** filters.

  <img className="hidden motion-reduce:block" src="/images/contacts/filtering-status.png" alt="Contacts with the Status filter open, showing Include and Exclude." />
</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the selection">
    Select **Apply** to update the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add detailed conditions">
    Open **Filters** to choose a field, condition, and value, then select **Apply Filters**.

    Use **Add Filter** for another condition. **And** requires both conditions; **Or** accepts either condition.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the sort order">
    Open **Sort** and choose a column and direction.

    Use **Add Sort** to break ties with another column. The control shows **Sorted By** after sorting is set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The date range limits results by when contacts were created. The column settings icon lets you choose visible columns from **Available** and reorder **Active** columns. **Fixed** columns stay in place.

## What you'll see

The table shows contacts matching your search, applied filters, date range, and access scope.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No contacts match">
    Select **Clear** in the filter bar, clear **Search**, and review the date range. These are separate controls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A colleague’s contact is missing">
    Clear **My Contacts** if it appears. Agents whose work location is set to Remote can only view their assigned contacts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The list didn’t change">
    Select **Apply** after changing quick filters, or **Apply Filters** after changing detailed conditions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
