Use the export tool to pull contacts out of PolicyDen as a CSV. You choose which contacts to export, which fields to include, and PolicyDen builds the file on the fly.

## Open the exporter

On the [Contacts page](/contacts/overview), click the export icon in the toolbar (to the left of the import and **Add Contact** buttons).

## Step 1: Pick what to export

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="All matching contacts">
    Exports every contact that matches the current search, filters, date range, and **My Contacts** toggle.

    The counter next to **All** shows exactly how many contacts will be included.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Selected contacts">
    Exports only the contacts you've selected with the checkboxes in the list.

    The counter next to **Selected** shows how many rows you've picked. This option is disabled until at least one contact is selected.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 2: Pick a format

CSV is the only format available. Your file downloads as `contacts.csv`.

## Step 3: Pick the fields

Choose which columns to include in the file. System fields like **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Date of Birth**, and **Medicare Number** are selected by default — the same defaults you see in the contact list.

You can:

- Search the field list to find a specific field quickly.
- Click **Select All** to include every available field (including custom fields).
- Click **Deselect All** to start from scratch.
- Remove a selected field by clicking the **×** on its pill above the list.

Every field your agency has defined as a [custom field](/customization/custom-fields) is available for export, not only the system ones.

## Step 4: Download

Click **Export**. PolicyDen builds the file and your browser starts the download.

<Note>
  Exports respect the filters and search that were active when you opened the dialog. If you change filters after opening the dialog, close and reopen it to pick up the new list.
</Note>

## What you get

Each row in the file represents one contact. The columns match the fields you selected, in the order you selected them. Dates are formatted as `MM/DD/YYYY`. Empty fields render as `-`.
