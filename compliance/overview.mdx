## Who sees what

Admins and Agency Owners in agencies with a Medicare product can open **Compliance** under **Management**.

Agency accounts can group results by **Agent**, **Date**, **State**, **Carrier**, or **SEP**. SEP means special enrollment period; see the [glossary](/start/glossary).

Individual agent accounts start on **Date** and have no **Agent** tab. Their fourth summary card is **Low-Risk Sales**, rather than **Below Threshold**.

## Read the summary

**High-Risk Sales** counts scored sales below 70%. **Avg Compliance Score** shows the average score. **Below Threshold** counts agents whose average falls below 80%.

The **Risk Level** column labels averages under 70% **Compliance Risk**, 70% to under 80% **Unacceptable**, 80% to under 90% **Acceptable**, and 90% or higher **Exceptional**.

**No compliance data** means there are no sales with completed scorecards in that range. Widen the dates or inspect the policy's available scorecard before expecting it in this summary.

Use scorecards to guide review; a score alone doesn't explain which requirements were missed. Open the policy or call details for that context.
