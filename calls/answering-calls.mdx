<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Answer queue calls from the header phone. Set yourself **Available** with **Inbound** selected before waiting for incoming calls.

## Before you start

- Confirm your phone shows **Connected**.
- Ask your Admin to confirm your queue assignment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the incoming call">
    Check the caller number, available contact details, queue, and any **Policy** details before the ring countdown ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the caller">
    Select **Accept**. Select **Reject** if you cannot take the call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage the conversation">
    Use **Mute** or **Unmute**, **Hold** or **Resume**, and **Keypad** as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="End the call">
    Select **End Call** when finished, then complete **Wrap Up**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Queues with Auto Answer connect you automatically with a beep. During a call, **Audio** opens audio controls. **View Script** appears when a script is available; call history provides earlier call context.

If you joined someone else’s call, the panel says **You are a participant on this call**. Use **Leave Call** when you’re done.

## What you'll see

The phone shows the active conversation, then asks for a disposition after the call ends.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Hold ends by itself">
    **Maximum hold time reached** means the call has been taken off hold.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You need to refresh">
    The **You’re on an active call** dialog offers **Stay on the Call** and **Refresh & End Call**. Stay to continue talking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No incoming call arrives">
    Check [Not getting calls](/fix/not-getting-calls) for status, queue, hours, cap, and connection checks.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
