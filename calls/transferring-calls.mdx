<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Use **Transfer** to hand off a caller, or **Add** to include another person. Available options depend on your queue or campaign.

## Before you start

- Start from an active call.
- Confirm the destination before selecting it: selecting a saved number or agent starts the action.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the transfer panel">
    In the active call, select **Transfer**, or select **Add** to open **Add to Call**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the destination type">
    Select **Numbers** for saved destinations, **Agents** for colleagues, or **Number** to enter a number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the handoff style">
    For number transfers, choose **Warm** to stay on the line or **Blind** to leave immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the destination">
    Select a saved destination or available agent. For a custom number, enter it and select **Transfer** or **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave when ready">
    After a warm handoff, use **Leave Call** when you’re ready to leave the conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only available or paused agents can receive transfers or be added. **Warm** and **Blind** choices apply to number transfers, not the **Agents** tab. A blind transfer leaves the caller hearing the destination ring.

Use the participant controls to **Hold**, **Resume**, or **Remove from call**. The **Caller** badge identifies the caller.

## What you'll see

A warm transfer or added person appears in the participant list; a blind transfer removes you from the conversation.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Transfer or Add is missing">
    **Transfer** needs configured transfer numbers or Agent Transfers. **Add** also depends on the participant limit. Ask your Admin to check the queue or campaign.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An agent is unavailable">
    Choose an available or paused agent. If no agents appear, ask your Admin to check queue or campaign assignments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
