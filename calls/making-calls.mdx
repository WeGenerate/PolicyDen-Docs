<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Use a contact’s saved number or enter a number yourself. Your browser phone must be connected before you start.

## Before you start

- Finish any pending [call disposition](/calls/wrapping-up-calls).
- Check your microphone and [phone connection](/calls/setting-your-status).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialpad">
    In the header, open **Calls**, then select **Dialpad**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the phone number">
    Enter the number you want to call.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the call">
    Select **Call**. The phone shows **Outgoing Call** and **Ringing...**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the conversation">
    Use **End Call**, then save the call’s outcome. While ringing, use **Cancel Call** to stop dialing.
  </Step>
</Steps>
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/calls/dialpad.png" alt="Calls menu with the Dialpad tab selected." />
</Frame>

You can also use **Call** on a contact profile, in a Messages conversation header, or on a call detail page. **Call back** appears in the header’s recent-call list.

For campaign calling, choose **Outbound** and a campaign in your status menu. The dialer places calls automatically and connects answered calls. Detected answering machines are ended automatically.

## What you'll see

The active call opens in your phone tab; **Calling...** tells you when it is running in another tab.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The number is blocked">
    “This number is on the Do Not Call list.” blocks dialing. Ask your Admin to review the record under [DNC Numbers](/dialer/dnc-numbers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Another call blocks dialing">
    “You are already on a call.” requires finishing that call. “Please disposition your last call before making a new call.” requires saving its outcome.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No calling number is available">
    “No phone numbers available for outbound calls.” means an Admin must check [Phone Numbers](/dialer/phone-numbers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your phone is not connected">
    Open the tab where your phone is connected, or refresh this page before trying again.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
