<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Dialer **Reporting** at `/dialer/reports` covers call results and agent time. Admin **Reporting** at `/reports` covers broader [agency reporting](/reports/overview).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open dialer reports">
    Select **Reporting** in the dialer navigation. Admins open it from the **Dialer** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the period">
    Use the date range picker to choose the period you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the report view">
    Select **Outcomes** for results or **Efficiency** for time and occupancy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the results">
    Apply the available filters and choose breakdown levels to group the results.
  </Step>
</Steps>
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/calls/reporting.png" alt="Dialer Reporting with Outcomes and Efficiency views." />
</Frame>

**Outcomes** includes calls, sales, callbacks, and conversion rate (**CVR**). **DNC** counts Do Not Call outcomes; see the [glossary](/start/glossary). **Efficiency** includes talk, wait, and pause time, averages, **Time Distribution**, and **Occupancy**.

Agents review their own results. Admins also see **Billable**, **Spend**, **CPA** (cost per acquisition), and **Telco** costs. Individual agent accounts do not have an **Agent** breakdown.

## What you'll see

The report groups matching calls or time totals by the selected breakdowns.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Results look incomplete">
    Check the date range and filters first. Your role also limits which calls and metrics appear.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A financial column is missing">
    **Spend**, **CPA**, and **Telco** are Admin views. Ask an Admin to review agency costs.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
