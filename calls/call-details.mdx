<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Open a call from **Call Log** to review its recording, transcript, and available scorecard. Agents can access their own calls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a call">
    In **Call Log**, select the call you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the conversation">
    Use the recording player when available, then read the transcript and scorecard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the outcome editor">
    Select **Edit disposition** beside the current outcome to open **Edit Disposition**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the corrected outcome">
    Choose the outcome and any required sold policy, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The header identifies the caller, source, time, and outcome. **Attached Policy** links to the associated policy when one exists. The **Call** control starts a new outbound call to that number.

A scorecard may be queued, processing, unavailable, or not applicable. Its absence does not prevent you from reviewing other details.

## What you'll see

The updated disposition appears in the call header after saving.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No recording or transcript appears">
    **No recording is available for this call.** means there is no audio to play. **Transcription queued** or **Transcribing recording** means processing is unfinished.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Processing failed">
    **Transcription failed** or **Scorecard failed** means that result could not be generated. Include the call identifier when contacting support.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The outcome cannot be saved">
    Choose a disposition and select a policy for a sale. If no choices appear, ask your Admin to check dispositions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
