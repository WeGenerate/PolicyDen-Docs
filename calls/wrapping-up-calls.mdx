<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

A disposition records how the call went. Complete **Wrap Up** before placing another call.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the finished call">
    In **Wrap Up**, confirm the caller and call details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the outcome">
    Under **How did the call go?**, select the disposition that matches the conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach the sold policy">
    For a sale outcome, select the policy under **Which policy was sold?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your next status">
    If **What’s your next status?** appears, choose **Available** or **Paused**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the disposition">
    Select **Save Disposition**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Outcome choices come from your queue or campaign. Choose the actual result rather than using a sale outcome to clear the panel. You can correct an outcome later with **Edit disposition** on [call details](/calls/call-details).

## What you'll see

The outcome is saved and your phone leaves the wrap-up panel.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Save Disposition is disabled">
    Choose an outcome first. A sale outcome also requires a selected policy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No dispositions are available">
    Ask an Admin to add dispositions to the queue or campaign. **Close** dismisses the empty panel; it does not select an outcome.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
