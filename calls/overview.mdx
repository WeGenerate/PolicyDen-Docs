The calls widget puts a phone on every page of PolicyDen. Dial a contact, answer an incoming call, and keep a record of what happened — without leaving the app or reaching for your cell.

## Before you start

Two things need to be in place before you can call:

- Your agency has at least one active SMS number, which doubles as your caller ID. Admins configure these in [SMS numbers](/settings/sms-numbers).
- Your browser has permission to use the microphone. The first time you place or take a call, you'll be prompted to allow mic access.

PolicyDen connects your browser to the phone system after your first click or key press on the page. A green indicator on the calls widget in the top bar means you're ready; a red one means there's a configuration issue.

## The calls widget

The calls widget lives in the top bar on every page. Click it to open a popover with your recent call history and controls for any active call.

What the widget shows depends on call state:

| State | What you see |
| ----- | ------------ |
| Idle | Your last few calls with play buttons for any recordings. |
| Incoming call | The caller's name, number, and linked policies with **Accept** and **Reject** buttons. |
| Outbound ringing | The contact you're dialing with an option to cancel. |
| On a call | A live timer, mute toggle, and **End Call** button. |

<Note>
  If you try to leave the page during an active call, PolicyDen asks you to confirm before navigating away so you don't accidentally drop the call.
</Note>

## Place an outbound call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the call from anywhere">
    You can call a contact from three places:

    - The **Call** button in the header of a message conversation.
    - A phone number on the [contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile).
    - The calls widget in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the contact to answer">
    PolicyDen picks the right caller ID automatically — the last agency number you texted the contact from, or your agency's default active SMS number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk">
    Once the contact picks up, the widget shows a running timer. Use **Mute** to silence your mic or **End Call** to hang up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Answer an incoming call

When a contact calls one of your agency's numbers, the calls widget turns into an incoming-call trigger and the popover opens with the caller's details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the caller">
    The widget shows the contact's name, the phone number, and any policies linked to that contact so you have context before you pick up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept or reject">
    Click **Accept** to take the call or **Reject** to send them away. Accepting triggers a mic permission prompt the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle the call">
    Once connected, the widget behaves like an outbound call — timer, mute, and **End Call**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If you reject a call, the contact hears the standard line-busy tone. There is no voicemail routing from the in-app dialer — if you expect voicemail, set that up on the carrier side.
</Warning>

## Call recordings

Calls that complete successfully are recorded and linked to the contact. To listen back:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the calls widget">
    Click the phone icon in the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the call">
    Recent calls appear with the contact's name, direction (incoming or outgoing), and timestamp. Missed calls are marked in red.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Play the recording">
    Calls with a recording show a play button and progress bar. Click **Play** to listen; click **Pause** to stop. Only one recording plays at a time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Recordings also appear on the contact's profile under the **Activities** tab, alongside texts and notes.

## Call history

The widget surfaces your five most recent calls. For a contact's complete call history — every inbound, outbound, and missed call — open their [contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile) and go to the **Activities** tab.

Each logged call captures:

- Who called whom (direction).
- Contact name and number.
- When it happened.
- The result — answered or missed.
- Duration, once the call ends.
- The recording, if one exists.
