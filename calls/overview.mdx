PolicyDen’s dialer lets you receive queue calls, place calls, and record outcomes in your browser. Your agency must have the dialer turned on.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/calls/orientation.png" alt="Dialer workspace with the phone status and Calls controls." />
</Frame>

The header has two controls. The status control is your phone: use it for availability, audio, and active calls. **Calls** contains **Calls**, **Missed**, and **Dialpad** tabs.

Keep your phone tab open. Allow microphone access and check that the status menu shows **Connected** before taking calls.

## Who sees what

| Role or account | Available views | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent | **Call Log**, **Reporting** | Your calls and results |
| Agent with Training turned on | **Live Monitor** too | Listen-only monitoring |
| Admin or Agency Owner | Separate **Dialer** panel | Agency calls and settings |
| Individual agent account | Dialer tools when enabled | No Live Monitor or Agent report breakdown |

Dialer [Reporting](/calls/reporting) covers calls. Admin [Reporting](/reports/overview) covers broader agency results.
