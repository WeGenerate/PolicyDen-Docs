<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Choose your availability before taking calls. You need microphone access and a connected browser phone.

## Before you start

- Connect your headset and keep your phone tab open.
- Ask your Admin which queues or outbound campaign you should use.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your phone">
    In the header, select your current status, such as **Offline** or **Paused**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow your microphone">
    Select **Allow microphone access**, then allow access in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your audio">
    Open **Audio Settings**. Choose your **Microphone** and **Speaker**, then use **Test Microphone** and **Test Speakers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose call direction">
    Under **Taking Calls**, select **Inbound** or **Outbound**. For outbound calling, use **Select a campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your availability">
    Under **Set your status**, select **Available** when the connection shows **Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/calls/status.png" alt="Phone menu showing status choices and Taking Calls." />
</Frame>

Use **Paused**, **Lunch**, **Training**, or **Offline** when you’re not ready for queue calls. **In Call** and **Wrap Up** are set automatically. Audio choices include **Default Microphone** and **Default Speaker**.

## What you'll see

Your header status changes to your selected availability.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No campaigns appear">
    “You’re not assigned to any active campaigns yet.” means an Admin needs to check your campaign assignment and whether it is active.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The phone is offline">
    For **Phone connection offline**, refresh the page to reconnect before going available. Avoid refreshing during a call.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your status changes to Paused">
    **You were paused automatically**, **Call cap reached**, and **Queues are closed** each explain a different interruption. Follow [Not getting calls](/fix/not-getting-calls).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
