<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Use **Call Log** to find a call, check its outcome, and open its details. Agents see their own answered or outgoing calls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open call history">
    In the sidebar, select **Call Log**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Use the date range picker to include the day you need. The initial range is today.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the list">
    Use **Search** and filters such as direction, source, disposition, or state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the call">
    Select a call row to view its details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
<Frame>
  <img src="/images/calls/log.png" alt="Call Log with date range, filters, and call rows." />
</Frame>

The summary cards include calls, sales, callbacks, and no answers. They help narrow the list to a particular result. Clear filters when you need to return to a wider set of calls.

Admins and Agency Owners can review agency calls. Their list also includes **Billable**, **Agent**, and **Vendor** columns, which Agents do not see.

## What you'll see

Matching calls appear in the list, with more rows loading as you scroll.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A call is missing">
    Expand the date range and clear search and filters. Agents only see calls assigned to them that were answered or outgoing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A recording is missing">
    Open the call details. A call without a recording shows **No recording is available for this call.**
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
