The columns on your pipeline board are stages you define. Agency admins can add new stages, rename or reorder the ones you already have, and delete stages you no longer need.

## Prerequisites

You need the **Admin** or **Agency Owner** role in your agency to open the configuration. Agents can see and use the board, but they can't change its structure. See [Users and roles](/settings/users-and-roles) for how roles are assigned.

## Open pipeline settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Pipeline">
    Click **Pipeline** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the settings icon">
    Click the **gear** icon in the top-right of the pipeline header. The **Configure Pipeline** dialog opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The dialog shows your pipeline name and every stage in order, each with a color label and a drag handle.

## Rename the pipeline

The **Pipeline Name** field at the top controls the label shown above the board. Edit the text and save to update it.

## Add a stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the plus button">
    Scroll to the bottom of the stage list and click the **+** button. A blank row appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the stage">
    Type the stage name — for example, **Application sent** or **Awaiting decision**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a color">
    Click the icon on the left of the row to open the color picker and choose a label color.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Pipeline**. The new column appears at the end of the board.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rename or recolor a stage

In the same dialog, click into a stage's name to edit it, or click its color icon to change the label color. Click **Save Pipeline** to apply your changes.

## Reorder stages

Grab the **drag handle** on the left of any stage row and drop it above or below other stages. The order in the dialog matches the left-to-right order of columns on the board.

## Delete a stage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the trash icon">
    Next to the stage you want to remove, click the **trash** icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Pipeline**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a stage removes it from the board and unsets the stage on every contact that was in it. Those contacts stay in PolicyDen, but they won't appear on the board until you assign them to another stage.
</Warning>

## Tips for a useful pipeline

<Tip>
  Keep stages short and action-oriented ("Quoted", "Application sent", "Enrolled"). Every stage should represent a real step in your sales process, not a status like "Active" or "Lost".
</Tip>

- Give related stages the same color so your team can scan the board quickly.
- Reorder stages to match the flow an agent works — leftmost column first, rightmost last.
- If a stage sits empty for weeks, delete it to keep the board focused.
