<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents for contacts assigned to them; Admins and Agency Owners for all agency contacts.</Info>

Use a pipeline to track a contact's progress. The contact and at least one pipeline stage must already exist.

## Add a deal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your pipeline">
    In **Pipelines**, open the board you want to work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the deal form">
    Select **Add Deal**. On a narrow screen, use the header's plus icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a stage">
    Under **Add to stage**, select the contact's current stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your contact">
    Under **Find Contact**, search using **Search by MBI or Name**, then select the matching contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the deal">
    Select **Add**. The contact appears in the selected stage.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A column's plus icon opens the same form with its stage already selected and locked. Adding a contact already on this board moves their existing card. A contact can belong to several pipelines, but only one stage per pipeline.

## Move a deal

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the card">
    Locate the contact's card in its current stage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag to another stage">
    Drag the card's body into the destination column, avoiding its name and action buttons.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release the card">
    Release the card in the column. PolicyDen saves the new stage automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add an activity or remove a deal

Select a card's plus icon to open **Add Activity**, then choose **Task**, **Meeting**, or **Note**. Follow the linked task pages for the full forms.

To remove a card, select its trash icon. In **Remove Deal?**, select **Remove**. The contact stays in PolicyDen.

## What you'll see

The card appears in its saved stage, and the column counts reflect the move.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Add is unavailable">
    Choose both a stage and a contact. If the board has no stages, ask an Admin or Agency Owner to configure it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The card moved back">
    **Failed to update stage** means the move wasn't saved. Try dragging it again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't find a contact">
    Agents can manage only contacts assigned to them. Check the assignment with your Admin or Agency Owner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
