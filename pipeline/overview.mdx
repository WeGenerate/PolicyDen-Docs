The **Pipeline** page shows every contact in your book as a card on a Kanban board. Each column is a stage you define, so you can see at a glance where every lead and client stands and drag them forward as they progress.

## Open the pipeline

Click **Pipeline** in the sidebar. You'll see your agency's Medicare Pipeline with one column per stage. A tag at the top shows your total lead count.

<Note>
  The pipeline is scoped to your agency — every user with access sees the same stages and the same contacts.
</Note>

## Reading a card

Each card on the board represents one contact.

| Detail | Where it comes from |
| ------ | ------------------- |
| Medicare number | The contact's MBI, shown above the name. |
| Name | The contact's full name. |
| Assigned agent | The agent who owns the contact, shown as a tag. |
| Product tag | The product the contact is associated with (for example, **Medicare Advantage**). |

Click a card's **+** icon to add an activity to that contact — a task, meeting, note, or file — without leaving the board.

## Move a contact between stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Grab the card">
    Click and hold any contact card. The cursor turns into a grab handle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag to a new column">
    Drop it into any other stage. The board scrolls if you drag to the edge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release to save">
    PolicyDen saves the new stage for you. If something goes wrong, the card returns to its original column and a toast explains why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also drag cards up and down inside a single column to reorder them for your own reference.

## Add a deal

You can drop an existing contact onto the board from two places:

- The **Add Deal** button in the top-right opens a picker where you choose a stage and search for a contact.
- The **+** icon at the top of any column opens the same picker with that stage pre-selected.

Search contacts by name or **MBI** (Medicare Beneficiary Identifier). Pick one, click **Add**, and the contact appears as a card in the chosen stage.

<Tip>
  Use **Add Deal** when you want to pull an existing contact into the pipeline. If the person isn't in PolicyDen yet, see [Creating contacts](/contacts/creating-contacts) first.
</Tip>

## Empty pipeline

If you haven't set up any stages yet, the board is empty. An admin needs to configure the stages before contacts can appear on the board.

See [Configuring the pipeline](/pipeline/configuring-pipeline) for how to add, rename, reorder, and delete stages.
