<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer enabled.</Info>

Configure how publishers check availability and send calls. Vendors can have pricing separate from the queue’s general availability ping.

## Before you start

- [Create a queue](/dialer/queues) and assign agents.
- [Create vendors and tags](/dialer/vendors-and-tags).

<Frame>
<img src="/images/dialer/pay-per-call.png" alt="Queue list where you open publisher settings." />
</Frame>

<Steps>
<Step title="Open queue billing">
  In **Dialer** → **Queues**, open your queue and find **Billing**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set general pricing">
  Turn on **Billable** if publishers use the general availability ping, then set pricing and caps.
</Step>
<Step title="Save billing settings">
  Select **Save** in **Billing**.
</Step>
<Step title="Attach publishers">
  In **Vendors**, select **Attach Vendors** and select your vendors.
</Step>
<Step title="Set vendor terms">
  Choose **Payout**, enter pricing and limits, then select **Attach Vendor** (or **Attach N Vendors**).
</Step>
<Step title="Share the vendor endpoint">
  Copy the vendor’s **Ping URL** for that publisher.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>Review prices before saving. Queue billing pays publishers using the general availability ping; attached vendors have their own pricing.</Warning>

## Billing and limits

| Setting | What it does | Default | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Billable** | Enables payments through the general availability ping. | Off without a price | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Billable After** | Answered duration needed for billing; custom values allow 0–3,600 seconds. | Check your queue | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Price per Billable Call** | Amount paid per qualifying call; must exceed $0. | Unset | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Count Caps By** | **All answered calls** or **Billable calls only** count toward agent caps. | Billable calls only | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **CPA Cap After** | Calls required today before an agent’s cost-per-acquisition cap applies. | Check your queue | Admin, Agency Owner |
| Vendor **Payout** | **Per call** or **Per conversion**. | Per call | Admin, Agency Owner |
| Vendor **Daily Cap** | Limits daily calls. | No cap | Admin, Agency Owner |
| Vendor **Concurrency Cap** | Limits simultaneous calls. | No cap | Admin, Agency Owner |
| Vendor **CPA Cap** | Limits cost per acquisition. | No cap | Admin, Agency Owner |

For **Per call**, set **Price per Billable Call** and **Billable After**. For **Per conversion**, set **Price per Conversion**. Vendor menus offer **Pause**, **Activate**, and **Remove Vendor**.

## Set availability rules

<Steps>
<Step title="Review the endpoint">
  In **Availability Ping**, copy **Endpoint** and open **Show example request**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose required data">
  Under **Params**, review required fields and use **Add Tag** for custom tags.
</Step>
<Step title="Set agent reservation">
  Choose **Agent Reservation**, then select **Save**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set rejection rules">
  In **Bid Rules**, enable rules and select their matching values.
</Step>
<Step title="Save rejection rules">
  Select **Save** in **Bid Rules**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Missing required parameters reject a ping. **Agent Reservation** reserves an agent after an available response for the selected duration.

**Reject By Disposition** checks prior call outcomes within **Within the Last**. **Reject By Policy Status** appears for supported queue products and checks selected statuses. **Reject Recently Billable** checks prior billable calls within the chosen period.

## What you'll see

Attached vendors show pricing, limits, status, and **Ping URL**.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="There are no vendors to attach">
  Create a vendor on the **Vendors** page first.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A rule will not save">
  Choose at least one disposition or policy status for the corresponding enabled rule.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Pings are rejected">
  Check required parameters, bid rules, agent availability, queue hours, and vendor limits.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
