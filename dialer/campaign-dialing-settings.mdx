<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Set how your campaign places calls and when it can run. Start with an [existing campaign](/dialer/campaigns).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your campaign">
    In **Dialer > Campaigns**, open the campaign you want to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the dialing mode">
    In **Dialing Mode**, choose **Power Dial** or **Predictive Dial**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/dialer/campaign-dialing-mode.png" alt="Campaign editor showing Dialing Mode settings." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the call pace">
    Review retry and timing settings, then select **Save** in **Dialing Mode**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Limit calling hours">
    In **Schedule**, turn on **Business Hours**, then set **Timezone** and **Hours**. Save that section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose call audio">
    In **Audio**, choose **Hold Music** and **Abandon Message**, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | What it does | Default | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Mode** | **Power Dial** calls one contact per free agent. **Predictive Dial** calls several. | Power Dial | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Lines Per Agent** | Sets 2–10 simultaneous calls per free agent in Predictive Dial. | 2 lines | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Max Attempts** | Limits calls to each contact, from 1–10 attempts. | 3 attempts | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Retry After** | Sets the wait after no answer; options range from 15 minutes to 1 day. | 4 hours | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Ring Timeout** | Sets how long to ring; options range from 15 seconds to 1 minute. | 30 seconds | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **After Call Status** | Sets agents to **Paused** or **Available** after they submit an outcome. | Paused | Admin, Agency Owner |

**Hold Music** plays while an agent places a contact on hold. **Abandon Message** plays when a contact answers without a free agent; the call then ends. Prepare recordings in the [Audio Library](/dialer/audio-library). The **Abandon Message** help text calls for your business name and a callback number.

<Warning>Review your agency's compliance requirements before changing predictive calling, calling hours, or abandonment audio.</Warning>

In **Transfers**, configure the destinations agents can use. See [Transfer calls](/calls/transferring-calls) for the agent's steps.

## What you'll see

The campaign uses your saved settings, and **Abandoned** shows an abandon rate alongside the count.

Campaign calls detect answering machines automatically. Detected machines are disconnected and marked **ANSWERING_MACHINE** without reaching an agent.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Lines Per Agent is hidden">
    This control appears only when **Predictive Dial** is selected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Calls happen outside expected hours">
    Check the saved schedule and time zone. With scheduling off, the campaign can dial at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An audio file is missing">
    Add it to the [Audio Library](/dialer/audio-library), then reopen the campaign.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
