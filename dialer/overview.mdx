<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer enabled.</Info>

The dialer sends incoming calls to queues and places outgoing calls through campaigns. In the sidebar, open **Management** → **Dialer** to manage your agency’s setup.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/dialer/overview.png" alt="Dialer navigation beside the agency queue list." />
</Frame>

## What you can do here

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Set up queues" href="/dialer/queues">Assign incoming numbers, agents, dispositions, and scripts.</Card>
<Card title="Set routing and hours" href="/dialer/queue-routing-and-hours">Control waiting audio, hours, and transfers.</Card>
<Card title="Manage phone numbers" href="/dialer/phone-numbers">Buy numbers and build caller ID pools.</Card>
<Card title="Set up campaigns" href="/dialer/campaigns">Prepare outbound calling.</Card>
<Card title="Monitor calls" href="/dialer/live-monitor">Listen and help agents during calls.</Card>
<Card title="Upload audio" href="/dialer/audio-library">Prepare messages and hold music.</Card>
</CardGroup>

Start with audio and numbers. Then prepare [dispositions](/dialer/dispositions) and [scripts](/dialer/scripts), create queues and campaigns, and assign agents.

**Management** also contains **Reporting** and **Call Log**. See [call reporting](/calls/reporting) and [call history](/calls/call-log). **Settings** includes **Vendors**, **Tags**, and **DNC Numbers** alongside your calling setup.

## Who sees what

| Role or account | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Admin or Agency Owner | Dialer setup and live supervision. |
| Agent | Calling tools, personal call history, and reporting. |
| Training agent | Listen-only Live Monitor, without summary stats, Spend, or CPA. |
| Individual agent account | No Live Monitor. |
