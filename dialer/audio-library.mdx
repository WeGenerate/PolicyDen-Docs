<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer enabled.</Info>

Upload audio once, then select it in queues or campaigns.

## Before you start

- Prepare an MP3 file no larger than 25 MB.
- Choose a name that explains its purpose.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the library">
  In **Dialer** → **Settings**, select **Audio Library**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the upload form">
  Select **Upload Audio File**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose your audio">
  Enter **Name**, then select the file area or drop your MP3 into it.
</Step>
<Step title="Upload the file">
  Select **Upload Audio** and wait for the upload to finish.
</Step>
</Steps>

<img src="/images/dialer/audio-library.png" alt="Audio Library showing uploaded files and preview controls." />

## Use your audio

| Location | Setting | Caller experience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Queue routing | **Wait Audio** | Loops while waiting for an agent. |
| Queue hold settings | **Hold Music** | Plays while on hold. |
| Queue schedule | **After Hours Audio** | Plays before the call ends outside business hours. |
| Campaign hold settings | **Hold Music** | Plays while on hold. |
| Campaign hold settings | **Abandon Message** | Plays when a contact answers without an available agent. |

## Change an existing file

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the file menu">
  In the audio list, open the file’s menu and select **Edit**.
</Step>
<Step title="Update the audio">
  Change **Name** or use the file area to choose a replacement MP3.
</Step>
<Step title="Save the change">
  Select **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

To remove a file, choose **Delete** from its menu and confirm **Delete Audio File**.

<Warning>Audio deletion cannot be undone. Review queues and campaigns using the file before deleting it.</Warning>

## What you'll see

The library shows your file’s name, duration, size, preview control, and date.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="The file is rejected">
  The upload form accepts MP3 files up to 25 MB.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The upload fails">
  Check your connection and retry. Keep the form open until the upload finishes.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
