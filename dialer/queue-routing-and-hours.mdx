<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer enabled.</Info>

Set when your queue accepts calls and what callers and agents experience.

## Before you start

- [Create a queue](/dialer/queues).
- [Upload audio](/dialer/audio-library) for waiting and after-hours messages.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/dialer/queue-routing-and-hours.png" alt="Queue list where you open a queue to change routing." />
</Frame>

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the queue">
  In **Dialer** → **Queues**, open your queue.
</Step>
<Step title="Set routing options">
  In **Routing**, choose your settings, then select **Save**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set business hours">
  In **Schedule**, turn on **Business Hours** and choose **Timezone**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set daily hours">
  Under **Hours**, enable working days, enter times, and use **Add break** where needed.
</Step>
<Step title="Set after-hours handling">
  Choose **After Hours Destination**, select **After Hours Audio** when required, then select **Save**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set hold options">
  In **Hold Settings**, choose **Hold Music** and **Max Wait Time**, then select **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Routing settings

| Setting | What it does | Default | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Accepted States** | Accepts selected states; empty means all states. | All states | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **State Licensing** | Rings only agents licensed in the caller’s state. | Off | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Auto Answer** | Connects an available agent immediately with a short beep. | Off | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **After Call Status** | Sets **Paused** or **Available** after the agent completes a disposition. | Check your queue | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Total Ring Timeout** | Total ringing time before No Answer. | 30 seconds | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Agent Ring Timeout** | Ringing time before trying the next available agent. | 15 seconds | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Wait for Agent** | Plays waiting audio until an agent is available, subject to **Max Wait Time**. | Off | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Wait Audio** | Required with **Wait for Agent**; loops while callers wait. | None | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Business Hours** | Limits availability; off means open 24/7. | Check your queue | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **After Hours Destination** | **Hangup** or **Play Audio & Hangup**. | Check your queue | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Hold Music** | Plays while callers are on hold. | None | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Max Wait Time** | Limits waiting or hold time. | Check your queue | Admin, Agency Owner |

## Add transfer destinations

<Steps>
<Step title="Enable agent transfers">
  In **Transfers**, turn on **Agent Transfers** to allow transfers to other available agents.
</Step>
<Step title="Add a destination">
  Select **Add New Transfer**, then choose a number or **Custom number**.
</Step>
<Step title="Label the destination">
  Enter a label and, for a custom destination, a ten-digit US number.
</Step>
<Step title="Save transfer settings">
  Select **Save**. Agents can use these destinations during calls.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Each section retains its saved settings for the queue.

## If it didn’t work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Waiting audio will not save">
  Choose **Wait Audio** when **Wait for Agent** is on.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Hours will not save">
  End times must follow start times. Keep breaks within the day’s hours and choose a timezone.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="After-hours audio is required">
  Choose a file when the destination is **Play Audio & Hangup**.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
