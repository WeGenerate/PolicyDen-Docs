<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Give agents consistent outcomes to select after calls. Flags control reporting, callbacks, and Do Not Call ([DNC](/start/glossary)) handling.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open disposition setup">
    In **Dialer > Dispositions**, select **Add Disposition**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/dialer/add-disposition-form.png" alt="Add Disposition form in the Dialer settings." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the outcome">
    Enter **Name** and, optionally, **Description**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose outcome flags">
    Turn on the flags that match this outcome. Review their effects before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the color">
    Review **Color** after changing flags; flag changes can reset it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the outcome">
    Select **Add Disposition**. For later changes, open the outcome and select **Save Disposition**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | What it does | Default | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Success** | Includes the outcome in success reporting. | Off | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Sale** | Counts the outcome as a sale in reports. | Off | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Final Status** | Keeps the contact out of the dialing queue. | Off | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **DNC** | Adds the phone number to the DNC list. | Off | Admin, Agency Owner |
| **Callback** | Marks a callback outcome. | Off | Admin, Agency Owner |

For reusable sets, select **Add Group** in **Disposition Groups**, enter **Disposition Group Name**, and choose its outcomes. Select **Add Disposition Group**. Assign a group or individual outcomes in a [queue](/dialer/queues) or [campaign](/dialer/campaigns).

## What you'll see

Agents see the assigned outcomes during [call wrap-up](/calls/wrapping-up-calls).

The dialer also supplies system outcomes: **IN_PROGRESS**, **CALLER_HUNGUP**, **ABANDONED**, **DROP**, **VOICEMAIL**, **ANSWERING_MACHINE**, **FAILED**, **NO_ANSWER**, **BUSY**, and **DNC**.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The name is already used">
    Choose another name. Disposition names must be unique within your agency.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A system outcome cannot be edited">
    System dispositions cannot be edited or deleted. Create an agency outcome when you need different behavior.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Agents cannot see the outcome">
    Check the queue or campaign's assigned dispositions, then save the selection.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
