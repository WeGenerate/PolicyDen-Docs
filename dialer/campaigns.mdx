<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Create a campaign to connect selected contacts with your agents. Set up a product and a number pool before adding it.

## Before you start

- Add the [product](/settings/products-and-carriers) you want to call about.
- Create a [number pool](/dialer/phone-numbers) with calling numbers.
- Decide which contacts and agents should take part.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open campaign setup">
    In **Dialer > Campaigns**, select **Add Campaign**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/dialer/create-campaign-form.png" alt="Add Campaign form in the Dialer settings." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose campaign details">
    Complete **Product**, **Name**, and **Number Pool**. Use a name that no other campaign uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the campaign">
    Select **Add Campaign**, then open the new campaign from the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your contacts">
    In **Contacts**, use **Select List** to choose a contact list, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign your agents">
    In **Users**, select the agents who should receive calls, then select **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set call outcomes">
    In **Dispositions**, choose the outcomes agents can use. Follow [Set up dispositions](/dialer/dispositions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review calling settings">
    [Configure campaign dialing](/dialer/campaign-dialing-settings), then review the **Active** or **Inactive** status at the campaign header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If no contact list exists, choose **Build A List** in **Contacts**. Enter a list name, add filters, and select **Save List**. **Matching** shows the eligible contact count. Do Not Call ([DNC](/start/glossary)) numbers and contacts on another running campaign are excluded.

## What you'll see

The campaign shows **In The List**, **Dialed**, **Left To Dial**, **Answered**, **Connected**, and **Abandoned** counts.

Assigned agents choose outbound calling and their campaign in the status widget. See [Set your status](/calls/setting-your-status).

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No number pools are available">
    Create a pool in [Phone Numbers](/dialer/phone-numbers), then reopen **Add Campaign**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The campaign has no matching contacts">
    Review the list filters, DNC list, and whether other running campaigns already include these contacts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An agent gets no calls">
    Check **Users**, campaign status, schedule, and the selected campaign. See [Not getting calls](/fix/not-getting-calls).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

To remove a campaign, use **Delete Campaign**. The confirmation warns that deletion can't be undone and removes campaign assignments.
