Start your first PolicyDen session by checking your account details and recording one follow-up. Each step links to the full guide if you need help.

## Before you start

Have your account email and sign-in details ready. If you received an invitation, [finish account setup](/getting-started/set-up-your-account) first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [app.policyden.com](https://app.policyden.com) and [sign in](/getting-started/sign-in) with your account email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your profile">
    Open **Profile** from your account menu and check your contact details. Follow [Update your profile](/settings/profile) to save any changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the contact you need">
    Open **Contacts** and find the person you need to follow up with. See [Find and open contacts](/contacts/overview). To add someone new, follow [Create a contact](/contacts/creating-contacts), which checks their phone number for an existing record first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record your next action">
    In **Tasks**, select **Add Task**. Enter a title, choose a due date, and select **Create Task**. Follow [Create a task](/tasks/creating-tasks) for the full steps, then [review your task list](/tasks/managing-tasks).
  </Step>
</Steps>

You now have a follow-up to return to in **Tasks**. Continue with [Work a new lead](/workflows/work-a-new-lead) or use the [app tour](/navigating-the-app) to find other workspaces.

## If you get stuck

[Fix sign-in problems](/fix/sign-in-problems) or [check a missing feature](/fix/cant-see-a-feature). For more help, email [support@policyden.com](mailto:support@policyden.com) with the page and what happened.
