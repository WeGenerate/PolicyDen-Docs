This guide is for new Agents. It walks you through your first session in PolicyDen — from signing in to logging your first contact, policy, and task. Expect it to take about 10 minutes.

If you're setting up your agency — inviting users, adding products, or paying a bill — start with [Agency settings overview](/settings/agency-overview) instead.

## Before you start

You need:

- An invitation email from your Admin, or an existing PolicyDen login.
- A modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge).
- Access to your work email, in case you need to verify or reset your password.

<Note>
  If you don't have an account yet, ask your Admin to invite you from **Agency settings → Users**.
</Note>

## Step 1: Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to [app.policyden.com](https://app.policyden.com) and sign in with your email and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept your invite">
    If this is your first time, click the link in your invitation email and set a password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an agency">
    If you work with more than one agency, choose the one you want to work in from the agency switcher in the top-left.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Set up your profile

Your profile controls how teammates and clients see you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar in the sidebar and choose **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a photo and contact info">
    Upload an avatar and confirm your name and work email. These appear on your signature and in call and SMS logs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your password">
    Under **Password**, set a strong password if you haven't already.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See [Your profile](/settings/profile) for every setting you can tune.

## Step 3: Create your first contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Contacts">
    Click **Contacts** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the new contact form">
    Click **Add Contact** in the top-right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the required details">
    PolicyDen needs **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Phone**, **Date of Birth**, and **Medicare Number** to save a new contact. Email, address, and any custom fields are optional at creation time and can be added later from the contact's profile. See [Create a contact](/contacts/creating-contacts) for the full field reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. PolicyDen opens the contact's profile so you can start working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  PolicyDen warns you if a contact with the same phone or email already exists, so you don't create duplicates.
</Tip>

Full details in [Create a contact](/contacts/creating-contacts).

## Step 4: Log a policy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    From the contact's profile, select the **Policies** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a policy">
    Click **Add Policy** and choose the product, carrier, and effective date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The policy appears under the contact and on the main **Policies** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="A saved policy opens into its full profile, with Policy Info and Policy Details cards on the contact.">
  <img src="/images/policy-profile-expanded.png" alt="Policy profile expanded on a contact showing Policy Info and Policy Details cards with carrier, product, sale date, effective date, and plan number" />
</Frame>

More in [Policies overview](/policies/overview).

## Step 5: Schedule a follow-up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tasks tab on the contact">
    From the contact's profile, select **Tasks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a task">
    Click **Add Task**, enter a title and due date, and assign it to yourself or a teammate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See it in your task list">
    The task shows up on the main **Tasks** page and on the contact's timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="The main Tasks page collects every follow-up across your team, filterable by status and assignee.">
  <img src="/images/tasks-list.png" alt="Tasks list with All, Open, In Progress, Completed, and Canceled status presets and a table of tasks with due dates, priority, status, and assignee" />
</Frame>

More in [Manage tasks](/tasks/managing-tasks).
