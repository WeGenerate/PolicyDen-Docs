<Info>**Who can do this:** Field Agents who own an individual agent account. You have Admin rights in your account.</Info>

Prepare your profile, products, licenses, and marketplace access for your first lead. Allow about 30 minutes.

## Before you start

- Have your license and carrier contract details ready.
- Have a payment card ready if you plan to take priced marketplace calls.

## 1. Complete your profile

Open **Profile** from the agency-name menu. Review **Details**, including your personal and license information.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-solo-account-1.png" alt="Profile details showing fictional personal and license information." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Set up your profile →](/settings/profile)

## 2. Set up products and carriers

In **Settings**, open **Products**. Add the products you sell and select their carriers.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-solo-account-2.png" alt="Set up products and carriers: Add Product control highlighted." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Set up products and carriers →](/settings/products-and-carriers)

## 3. Set licenses and contracts

On your own **Profile**, open **Licensed States** and **Contracts**. Set your states for each product and select the carriers and uplines you write through.

Your individual agent account has no **Users** or **API Keys** pages.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-solo-account-3.png" alt="Set licenses and contracts: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Set licensed states and contracts →](/settings/licensed-states-and-contracts)

## 4. Prepare marketplace calls

In **Wallet**, add funds for priced calls. Then open **Marketplace**, review queue pricing, and select **Join queue**.

[Full steps: Add wallet funds →](/settings/wallet-funds)

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-solo-account-4.png" alt="Marketplace queue cards showing prices, availability, and access controls." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Join queues →](/marketplace/joining-queues)

## 5. Work your first lead

Find or add the contact, record your conversation, and set a follow-up. Add the policy after completing your enrollment process.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-solo-account-5.png" alt="Add New Contact dialog with the Phone field highlighted." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Work a new lead →](/workflows/work-a-new-lead)

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No carriers appear when I add a policy">
    Check the product’s carriers and your **Contracts** selections. [Review contracts](/settings/licensed-states-and-contracts).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Marketplace calls are paused">
    Check wallet funds, queue membership, licensed states, and availability. [Fix missing calls](/fix/not-getting-calls).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
