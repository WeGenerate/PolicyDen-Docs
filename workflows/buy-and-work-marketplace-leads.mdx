<Info>**Who can do this:** Field Agents with their own account, and Admins or Agency Owners in marketplace agencies with the dialer enabled.</Info>

Fund the wallet, enable queue access, work calls, and track charges. Allow about 15 minutes before your first call.

## Before you start

- Enable the products you sell.
- Keep agents’ licensed states current for each product.
- Have a payment card ready for priced calls.

## 1. Fund the wallet

In **Settings**, open **Wallet** and select **Add Funds**. Review the amount and payment method before confirming.

<Warning>Adding funds charges your card. Priced marketplace calls draw from this prepaid balance.</Warning>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/buy-and-work-marketplace-leads-1.png" alt="Fund the wallet: Add Funds control highlighted." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Add wallet funds →](/settings/wallet-funds)

## 2. Choose queue access

In **Marketplace**, review queue prices, hours, and coverage. In an individual agent account, select **Join queue**; in an agency, use **Manage Access** to assign agents.

[Full steps: Assign queue access →](/marketplace/managing-queue-access)

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/buy-and-work-marketplace-leads-2.png" alt="Choose queue access: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Join marketplace queues →](/marketplace/joining-queues)

## 3. Work incoming calls

Set **Taking Calls** to **Inbound** and become **Available**. Handle each call, then record its disposition and next action.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/buy-and-work-marketplace-leads-3.png" alt="Work incoming calls: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Run a dialer shift →](/workflows/run-a-dialer-shift)

## 4. Check charges and balance

Return to **Wallet** to review **Balance**, **Charged**, and **Billable Calls**. In **Transactions**, review each charge and its remaining balance.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/buy-and-work-marketplace-leads-4.png" alt="Check charges and balance: Transactions control highlighted." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Review transactions →](/settings/wallet-transactions)

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A queue shows No funds">
    Add funds for priced calls. Free queues can continue receiving calls. [Fix wallet issues](/fix/wallet-and-payments).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Queue access is on, but calls do not arrive">
    Check hours, licensed states, and agent availability. [Fix missing calls](/fix/not-getting-calls).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
