<Info>**Who can do this:** Agency Agents on the Telesales channel, in agencies with the dialer enabled.</Info>

Take calls, record outcomes, plan callbacks, and review your shift. Allow about 10 minutes to prepare, then repeat for each call.

## Before you start

- Allow microphone access and connect your headset.
- An Admin must assign the queues or active campaign you need.

## 1. Prepare to take calls

Open your status menu and check **Audio Settings**. Under **Taking Calls**, choose **Inbound** or **Outbound**; outbound calling also needs a campaign.

Select **Available** when ready to receive calls.



[Full steps: Set your status →](/calls/setting-your-status)

## 2. Take the call

Select **Accept** for an incoming call. Review the caller’s details and record notes on their contact.



[Full steps: Answer calls →](/calls/answering-calls)

{/* TODO: verify the demo Wrap Up fixture before capturing phase 3; use a seeded ended call. */}

## 3. Finish and record the outcome

In **Wrap Up**, choose the disposition that matches the call. A sale disposition requires a policy under **Which policy was sold?**

Select **Save Disposition** and choose **Available** or **Paused** when the next-status selector appears.



[Full steps: Wrap up calls →](/calls/wrapping-up-calls)

## 4. Plan callbacks

Create a dated task on the contact when you promise another call. Use **Call Log** and **Callbacks** to review calls with callback outcomes.

## 5. Review the shift

Open **Reporting** for your call results, then set **Offline** when finished. This is dialer Reporting at `/dialer/reports`; agency Reporting at `/reports` is separate.

[Full steps: Understand agency Reporting →](/reports/overview)

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I am not getting calls">
    Check microphone access, status, and queue or campaign assignment. [Fix missing calls](/fix/not-getting-calls).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot save the outcome">
    Select a disposition and, for a sale, its policy. If no dispositions appear, ask an Admin to configure the queue or campaign.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
