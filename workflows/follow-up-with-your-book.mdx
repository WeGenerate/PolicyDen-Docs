<Info>**Who can do this:** Field Agents and Agency Agents with access to their assigned contacts.</Info>

Keep tasks, appointments, conversations, and policy follow-ups current. Allow about 30 minutes each week.

## Before you start

- Have access to the contacts you manage.
- Texting requires an active agency texting number.

## 1. Review the week’s tasks

In **Tasks**, review **To Do** and **In Progress** work. Open each task to check its due date and contact.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/follow-up-with-your-book-1.png" alt="Tasks table with status filters, due dates, and assigned agents." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Manage tasks →](/tasks/managing-tasks)

## 2. Check appointments

Open a contact’s **Activities** tab to review their meetings. Use **Add Meeting** when the next conversation needs an appointment.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/follow-up-with-your-book-2.png" alt="Check appointments: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Schedule meetings →](/meetings/scheduling-meetings)

## 3. Continue conversations

In **Messages**, review **Unread** conversations and respond. For a first text, open the contact’s **Messages** tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/follow-up-with-your-book-3.png" alt="Continue conversations: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Send texts →](/messages/sending-texts)

## 4. Review policy progress

In **Policies**, review statuses and open records needing attention. Ask an Admin about status corrections; Agents cannot change policy status.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/follow-up-with-your-book-4.png" alt="Policies table with status totals and fictional policy records." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Read policy statuses →](/policies/status-history)

## 5. Set the next follow-up

In **Pipelines**, update deal stages after conversations. Create the next task before marking finished work **Done**.
