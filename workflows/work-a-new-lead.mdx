<Info>**Who can do this:** Agency Agents and Field Agents. Calling requires the dialer to be enabled.</Info>

Take a lead from first contact to a recorded policy and an updated pipeline. Allow about 25 minutes with a ready client.

## Before you start

- Have the lead’s phone number and access to their contact record.
- Your product and carrier must be available before you add the policy.

## 1. Find or add the contact

In **Contacts**, search for the lead. If needed, select **Add Contact** and search their phone number before creating a record.

  <img src="/images/workflows/work-a-new-lead-1.png" alt="Add New Contact dialog with the Phone field highlighted." />

[Full steps: Find or add a contact →](/contacts/creating-contacts)

## 2. Reach the lead

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In person">

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="On the dialer">

[Full steps: Set your calling status →](/calls/setting-your-status)

## 3. Record the next action

On the contact, use **Add Note** to record the conversation. Use **Add Task** for a follow-up or **Add Meeting** for an appointment.

[Full steps: Schedule a meeting →](/meetings/scheduling-meetings)

## 4. Record the policy

After submitting the application through your enrollment process, select **Add Policy** in PolicyDen. Recording it starts Life Insurance at **Submitted** and Medicare at **Unknown**; it does not submit an application.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/work-a-new-lead-4.png" alt="Record the policy: Add Policy control highlighted." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Add the policy →](/policies/creating-policies)

## 5. Update the pipeline

In **Pipelines**, move the contact’s deal to the stage that matches the outcome. Use your agency’s stage names.

  <img src="/images/workflows/work-a-new-lead-5.png" alt="Pipeline board showing fictional deals organized by sales stage." />
</Frame>
