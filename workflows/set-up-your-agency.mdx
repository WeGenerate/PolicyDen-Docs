<Info>**Who can do this:** Agency Admins and Agency Owners. Dialer and marketplace phases depend on your agency’s enabled features.</Info>

Prepare your agency for agents to start selling. Allow about 45 minutes, plus time to gather team information.

## Before you start

- Sign in to the agency you want to configure.
- Gather product, carrier, and team details.
- Decide which agents will work in person or take dialer calls.

## 1. Review agency settings

Open **Settings** under **Management**. Confirm the active agency before changing products, relationships, or users.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-agency-1.png" alt="Review agency settings: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Review agency settings →](/settings/agency-overview)

## 2. Set products and relationships

In **Products**, add your products and select carriers. Review **Relationships** for the agencies and uplines you work with.

[Full steps: Review relationships →](/settings/relationships)

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-agency-2.png" alt="Set products and relationships: Add Product control highlighted." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Set products and carriers →](/settings/products-and-carriers)

## 3. Add users and fields

In **Users**, add teammates with the appropriate role and sales channel. In **Custom Fields**, configure the information your team needs.

[Full steps: Configure custom fields →](/settings/agency-custom-fields)

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-agency-3.png" alt="Add User dialog with the Role field highlighted." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Add users and roles →](/settings/users-and-roles)

## 4. Prepare calling access

If the dialer is enabled, configure inbound queues or outbound campaigns and assign agents. Check the phone numbers and dispositions needed for those calls.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/set-up-your-agency-4.png" alt="Prepare calling access: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Set up the dialer →](/dialer/overview)

## 5. Set up payments

Open **Billing** to add a payment method and review invoices. Marketplace agencies see **Wallet** instead; add funds before taking priced calls.

[Full steps: Add wallet funds →](/settings/wallet-funds)



[Full steps: Review billing →](/settings/billing)

## 6. Send agents their starting point

Share this message with your team:

> Start here: [Your first session in PolicyDen](/quickstart). Sign in, review your profile, and record a follow-up. Then [work your first lead](/workflows/work-a-new-lead).

Use the onboarding guide to finish each agent’s licenses and calling access.
