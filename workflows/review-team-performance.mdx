<Info>**Who can do this:** Agency Admins and Agency Owners. Dialer views require the dialer; Compliance requires a Medicare product.</Info>

Review sales, call results, and coaching priorities across your team. Allow about 20 minutes each week.

## Before you start

- Choose the reporting period and product you want to review.
- Use comparable date ranges when moving between screens.

## 1. Start with the dashboard

Open **Dashboard** and set the date range. Review sales and at-risk policies, then compare agents in **Leaderboard**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/review-team-performance-1.png" alt="Start with the dashboard: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Read the dashboard →](/dashboard/overview)

## 2. Open the live leaderboard

From **Leaderboard**, select **Open Live View**. Review **Total Sales:** and the agent ranking for the selected date range.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/review-team-performance-2.png" alt="Open the live leaderboard: Open Live View control highlighted." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Use the live leaderboard →](/dashboard/live-leaderboard)

## 3. Break down policy results

Under **Management**, open **Reporting** at `/reports`. Select **Product**, the date range, and **Group By** to investigate team results.

For Medicare, review **Persistency** when you need to compare continued coverage.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/review-team-performance-3.png" alt="Break down policy results: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Build an agency report →](/reports/building-reports)

## 4. Compare call results

In the **Dialer** panel, open **Reporting** at `/dialer/reports`. Review **Outcomes** and **Efficiency** for the same period.

This is separate from [agency Reporting](/reports/overview), which summarizes policies.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/review-team-performance-4.png" alt="Compare call results: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Read dialer Reporting →](/calls/reporting)

## 5. Choose live coaching

In **Live Monitor**, review **Agents**, **Queues**, **States**, and **Campaigns**. Use **States** to check inbound availability by state, then select a state's agent count to see its agents. Listen to a call when you need context for coaching.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/review-team-performance-5.png" alt="Choose live coaching: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Monitor live calls →](/dialer/live-monitor)

## 6. Review compliance concerns

For Medicare agencies, open **Compliance** and review **High-Risk Sales**. Open the policy’s scorecard to understand the result before choosing a follow-up.

Only policies with completed call scorecards contribute to these results.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/review-team-performance-6.png" alt="Review compliance concerns: current workspace and navigation." />
</Frame>

[Full steps: Review compliance →](/compliance/overview)

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Compliance has no data">
    Check the product, date range, and whether the calls have completed scorecards. [Review Compliance](/compliance/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The call and policy reports look different">
    They measure different activity. Confirm the product and date range in each view, then inspect the underlying calls or policies.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
