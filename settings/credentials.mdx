<Info>**Who can do this:** All users can manage their own credentials. Admins and Agency Owners can manage credentials on accessible user profiles.</Info>

Save logins for the platforms you use. Revealing a password or editing a saved login may ask for your PolicyDen password.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open saved credentials">
    In **Profile**, select **Credentials**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/settings/credentials-overview.png" alt="Profile Credentials tab with Add highlighted." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a login">
    In **Credentials**, select **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the login details">
    Enter **Platform**, **Username**, and **Password**. Add **Website** or extra fields if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the credential">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To reveal a saved password, select **Show password** and complete password confirmation if prompted. It hides again after 30 seconds. Use **Copy username** or **Copy password** to copy a value.

Use the credential’s actions menu to select **Edit** or **Delete**. Editing requires access to the saved secret. Deleting removes the stored login and cannot be undone.

## What you'll see

Your platform appears in the list, with its password hidden until you reveal it.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I cannot reveal or edit a login">
    If you are logged in as another user, switch back to your own account first. Revealing credentials is blocked during impersonation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No credentials match">
    Clear **Search** or search for the platform, username, website, or an extra field label.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The login will not save">
    Complete the required fields and check the field errors. When editing, leave **Password** empty to keep its current value.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
