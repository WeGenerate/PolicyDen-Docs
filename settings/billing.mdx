<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners at agencies that don't use the marketplace. Marketplace agencies use [Wallet](/settings/wallet).</Info>

Review agency charges and maintain the card used for billing. Check the amount and invoice before making a payment.

## Add a payment card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing">
    In **Settings**, select **Billing**.


  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the card form">
    In **Payment Methods**, select **Add Card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter billing details">
    Complete **Card Holder** and **Billing Address**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter card details">
    Complete **Card Number**, **Expiry**, and **CVC**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the card">
    Select **Add Card**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A card's row menu offers **Make Default** for a non-default card, and **Delete**. The current billing card has a **Default** badge.

## Pay an invoice

**Pay Now** appears on overdue, unpaid invoices. **Pay Invoice** appears in the row menu for open, unpaid invoices.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the invoice">
    In **Invoices**, review the amount and status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the payment">
    Select **Pay Now**, or select **Pay Invoice** from the invoice's row menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Review the invoice's status after payment completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use **Download PDF** from an invoice's row menu when a PDF is available.

<Warning>The **Payment Required** banner says, “Please pay now to avoid account suspension.” Review unpaid invoices when it appears.</Warning>

## What you'll see

Billing shows **Price Per Agent**, its billing day, **Total Overdue**, and **Total Paid**, plus your cards and invoices.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Billing isn't in Settings">
    Marketplace agencies see **Wallet** instead. Open [Wallet](/settings/wallet) for funds, cards, and transactions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="There are no invoices">
    **No Invoices yet** means invoices haven't appeared. The page states they appear once billing begins.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Billing data won't load">
    If **Failed to load billing data.** appears, contact support before relying on the summary amounts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The card form won't load">
    If **Stripe is not configured.** appears, contact support@policyden.com. Include your agency name and the error.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
