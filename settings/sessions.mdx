<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners can update their own profile.</Info>

Use Sessions to review your sign-in history and identify your current device. This tab shows records; it has no session-removal control.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your sessions">
    In **Profile**, select **Sessions**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/settings/sessions-overview.png" alt="Profile Sessions tab with sign-in history." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your current device">
    Look for **This device** beside the current session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the sign-in details">
    Review each device, sign-in time, IP address, and sign-in method when shown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Load older sessions">
    Scroll through the session list to load more records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The list shows recorded sessions, including a sign-out reason and time when available.

A sign-in method helps distinguish how access began. A recorded IP address may belong to your office network or internet provider. Compare unexpected entries with when and where you used PolicyDen.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No sessions appear">
    The empty state says “No sessions yet. Your sign-ins will show up here.” Revisit after signing in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The list will not load">
    Retry when the page reports that sessions could not load.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I do not recognize a sign-in">
    [Change your password](/settings/password) and contact your Admin or support@policyden.com with the time and device shown. Do not include passwords.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
