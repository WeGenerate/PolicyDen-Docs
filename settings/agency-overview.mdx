Agency settings control your products, carrier relationships, team access, and connected tools. Open **Settings** from the sidebar’s **Management** group; **Relationships** opens first.

<CardGroup cols={2}>

## Who sees what

Admins and Agency Owners can open agency settings. Agents use their [personal settings](/settings/profile) for their own details.

Individual agent accounts have Admin rights, but don't show **Users** or **API Keys**. Their own profiles include **Licensed States** and **Contracts**.

Agencies using the marketplace see **Wallet**. Other agencies see **Billing**. Use the section shown for your agency.
