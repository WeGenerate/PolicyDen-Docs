Agency settings is the Admin-only hub for everything that shapes how your team works in PolicyDen. Use it to invite users, set up the products you sell, define custom fields, manage SMS numbers and templates, and keep billing current.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/agency-settings-overview.png" alt="Agency settings side navigation with Products, Users, Custom Fields, Billing, SMS Numbers, and Roles & Permissions links" />
</Frame>

<Info>
  Agency settings are visible to users with the **Admin** or **Agency Owner** role. Agents don't see these pages.
</Info>

## Open agency settings

Click your avatar in the sidebar to switch into the Settings panel, then pick any item under **Agency**.

## A typical setup order

If you're setting up a new agency, work through the admin sections in this order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Add each teammate under [Users and roles](/settings/users-and-roles) so they can sign in and start working.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add products and carriers">
    Decide which product lines and carriers you write under [Products and carriers](/settings/products-and-carriers). This drives the options available on every policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure custom fields">
    Use [Custom fields](/settings/agency-custom-fields) to add any contact or policy fields your team tracks beyond the defaults.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up messaging">
    Provision an [SMS number](/settings/sms-numbers) and create reusable [templates](/settings/templates) for your team to send.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the pipeline">
    Set up the stages your Agents will work contacts through on the board. See [Configure the pipeline](/pipeline/configuring-pipeline).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add billing details">
    Add a card and confirm your invoices under [Billing](/settings/billing).
  </Step>
</Steps>
