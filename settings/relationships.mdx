<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners, including Admins of individual agent accounts.</Info>

Use **Relationships** to record the agencies you write through and manage carrier sharing. This is the default agency settings page.

## Add an upline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Relationships">
    In **Settings**, select **Relationships**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/agency-settings/relationships.png" alt="Relationships page showing Uplines, Downlines, Agents, and Partnerships." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the upline form">
    In **Uplines**, select **Add Upline**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the upline">
    Enter **Upline Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose commission type">
    Select **Commission Type**: **As Earned** or **Heap**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the upline">
    Select **Add Upline**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite a partner

Agencies see **Downlines**, **Agents**, and **Partnerships**. Individual agent accounts instead see **Referrals**, listing referred agents and their invitation status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation">
    In **Partnerships**, select **Send Invitation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter agency details">
    Complete **Agency Name** and **Commission Type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter owner details">
    Complete **Owner First Name**, **Owner Last Name**, and **Owner Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Select **Send Invitation**. A new owner receives an activation email if they aren't on PolicyDen yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For incoming invitations, select **Accept** from the row menu. Map offered products under **Map to your product**, then select **Accept Invitation**.

Use **Manage Carriers** where offered to review or change carrier sharing. Use **Add Downline** to invite a downline agency or **Add Agent** for an agent relationship. In **Agents**, select **Edit Referral** to choose the referring agent or **No referrer**, then select **Save**.

For a manually added upline, use **Edit** to update its details. Use **Remove** when ending a relationship.

The [policies list](/policies/overview) includes **Upline** and **Commission Type** columns for reviewing a policy’s relationship.

## What you'll see

Relationships appear in their sections, with invitation status and shared carriers where available.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't accept an invitation">
    When carriers are offered, create your agency products first and map every offered product before accepting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No carriers are available to share">
    Accept an upline invitation or ask your upline to offer carriers first.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An invitation hasn't been accepted">
    Use **Resend Invitation** when the row menu offers it. **Cancel Invitation** removes a pending invitation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
