<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners can update their own profile.</Info>

Keep your name, contact details, and timezone current in your personal profile. Each section saves separately.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    In your account menu, select **Profile**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/settings/profile-overview.png" alt="Profile Details tab with the Personal section." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your details">
    In your profile, select **Details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update personal information">
    In **Personal**, update **Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Date of Birth**, or **Timezone**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    In the changed section, select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also update license details, address, and banking information, or keep files in **Documents**. National Producer Number (NPN) identifies your producer record; see the [glossary](/start/glossary).

In an individual agent account, phone, birth date, NPN, resident state, and address fields are required when saving their sections.

## What you'll see

Your saved details appear in the profile; unchanged sections keep their existing values.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My changes were rejected">
    Check the field messages. Enter a valid, unused email address and a birth date in the past.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My email is unverified">
    If the profile shows **Resend verification email**, select it and follow the link in your inbox.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The profile will not load">
    Retry the load. If it still fails, contact support@policyden.com with the error shown.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
