Your profile is how teammates and clients see you inside PolicyDen. Keep it up to date so your name and photo appear correctly on activities, messages, and call logs.

</Frame>

## Open your profile

Click your avatar in the sidebar and choose **Profile**. The profile page opens in **Settings**.

## Profile photo

Your photo shows next to every message you send, every call you make, and every task you create.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the upload dialog">
    Hover your avatar at the top of the page and click the camera icon, or click **Upload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an image">
    Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP file up to 2 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The photo uploads immediately and you see an **Avatar updated successfully** confirmation. Your new photo appears across the app on the next page load.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To remove your photo, click **Remove**. You go back to the default avatar until you upload a new one.

## Name and email

The **Profile Information** card holds your core contact info. All three fields are required.

| Field | Where it appears |
| ----- | ---------------- |
| **First Name** | Shown in the sidebar, on activity feeds, and in shared views. |
| **Last Name** | Paired with your first name wherever your name appears. |
| **Email address** | Used to sign in and to receive notifications. Must be unique across PolicyDen. |

Edit any field and click **Save Profile**. A **Saved.** confirmation appears when the update goes through.

<Warning>
  Changing your email address signs you out of other sessions and may require you to re-verify the new address.
</Warning>

## Update your password

The same page has an **Update Password** card for setting a new password without leaving Settings. For the full walk-through, including password requirements, see [Password](/settings/password).

## Delete your account

The **Delete Account** card at the bottom of the profile page permanently removes your account and all of its data. You must confirm your current password to proceed.

<Warning>
  Deleting your account is final. If you manage an agency, hand off ownership or contact support before deleting.
</Warning>
