You can change your password any time from your profile page. Use a long, unique password and rotate it if you think it has been exposed.

## Update your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click your avatar in the sidebar and choose **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll to Update Password">
    The card sits below your profile information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    Type your existing password into **Current password**. You can click the eye icon to reveal what you typed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Enter your new password in **New password** and again in **Confirm password**. Both must match.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save password**. You see a **Saved.** confirmation when the change goes through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Password requirements

PolicyDen enforces these password rules:

- At least 8 characters.
- Not a password that has appeared in a known data breach.

<Tip>
  Use a password manager to generate a long random password and store it safely.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I get a 'current password is incorrect' error">
    Your **Current password** must match the password you use to sign in today. If you don't remember it, sign out and use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The page stops accepting my new password">
    PolicyDen limits password-update attempts to six per minute. Wait a minute and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I never received the password-reset email">
    Check your spam folder. If it still isn't there, ask your agency admin to confirm the email on your account, or contact [support](mailto:support@policyden.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
