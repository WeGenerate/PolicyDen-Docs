Templates save you from retyping the same message. Create an SMS or email template once, drop in placeholders for contact or policy details, and your team can send a personalized message in a couple of clicks.

<Info>
  Templates are managed by Admins. Every Agent in your agency can use them.
</Info>

## Email templates

### Create an email template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Email Templates page">
    From settings, navigate to **Email Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Email Template">
    The **Add New Email Template** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a type">
    Choose the event the template covers. For example, **Policy Submitted** is sent when a policy moves into a submitted state.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the subject">
    Enter the email **Subject**. You can drop tags into the subject using the tag buttons below the body.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the body">
    Enter the email **Body**. Tags from the **Available Tags** list below insert at your cursor — handy for personalizing the message with the contact's name, the policy number, and so on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Email Template**. The template is now available across your agency.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Enable or disable an email template

Each template has an active toggle on its row. Turn it **Off** to pause the template without deleting it. Turn it back **On** when you're ready to use it again.

### Edit or delete an email template

Use the actions menu (three-dot button) on any row:

- **Edit Email Template** — adjust the subject, body, or tags.
- **Delete Email Template** — remove the template for good.

## SMS templates

SMS templates work the same way as email templates, with one extra constraint: each message must fit within **160 characters**. PolicyDen shows a live character count as you type and warns you if you go over.

### Create an SMS template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SMS Templates page">
    From settings, navigate to **SMS Templates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add SMS Template">
    The **Add New SMS Template** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a type">
    Choose the event the template fires on. For example, **Policy Submitted**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the text">
    Type your message. Click tag buttons below the text box to insert placeholders like the contact's first name or policy number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep an eye on the count">
    Watch the counter on the right. If you go over 160 characters the count turns red and the **Create** button is disabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create SMS Template**. The template is ready for your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Enable, edit, or delete an SMS template

SMS templates share the same toggle and actions menu as email templates — use the toggle to pause a template, **Edit SMS Template** to change it, or **Delete SMS Template** to remove it.

## Example templates

Use these as a starting point — swap the tag names if your agency has renamed them, and adjust the tone to match how your team sounds.

### Example email template: policy submitted

Event type: **Policy Submitted**.

<CodeGroup>

```text Subject
{{contact.first_name}}, we submitted your {{policy.product}} application
```

```text Body
Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Good news — we just submitted your {{policy.product}} application with {{policy.carrier}}. Carriers usually return a decision within 10 business days. We'll email you the moment we hear back.

In the meantime, keep an eye out for a welcome letter from {{policy.carrier}} (it means the application was accepted) and feel free to reply to this email with any questions.

Thanks,
{{agent.first_name}} at {{agency.name}}
{{agent.phone}}
```

</CodeGroup>

### Example SMS template: enrollment confirmation

Event type: **Policy Submitted**. Under 160 characters so it sends as a single SMS.

```text SMS body
Hi {{contact.first_name}}, this is {{agent.first_name}} at {{agency.name}}. Your {{policy.product}} app was submitted today. I'll text when {{policy.carrier}} replies. Reply STOP to opt out.
```

<Tip>
  Tag names in your agency's instance may differ slightly. Use the **Available Tags** buttons below the body to insert the exact placeholders PolicyDen recognizes.
</Tip>

## Tips for writing templates

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use tags generously" icon="tag">
    Placeholders like the contact's first name or policy number make a template feel personal. Every place you can add free text, you can drop a tag.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Keep SMS short and direct" icon="message-square">
    You have 160 characters. Say who you are, what you need, and how to reply. Save the long pitch for email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Always include an opt-out on SMS" icon="shield-alert">
    Regulated outreach — including Medicare and ACA compliance — expects a clear opt-out. End SMS templates with **Reply STOP to opt out** or your agency's approved language.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Test before you rely on it" icon="flask-conical">
    Send the template to your own phone or email first to make sure the tags fill in the way you expect.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
