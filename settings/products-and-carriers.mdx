<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners, including Admins of individual agent accounts.</Info>

Set up the products your agency sells, then assign carriers to each product. You can pause products without removing them.

## Add a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Products">
    In **Settings**, select **Products**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/agency-settings/products.png" alt="Products and Carriers sections, with Add Product highlighted." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a product">
    Select **Add Product**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a template">
    Select **Medicare**, **Life Insurance**, or **Custom Product**. For a custom product, enter **Product Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the product">
    Select **Add Product** in the dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assign carriers

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the carrier form">
    In **Carriers**, select **Add Carrier**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the carrier">
    Enter **Carrier Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the carrier">
    Select **Add Carrier**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose product carriers">
    On the product row, open **Select carriers** and select the carriers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your choices">
    Select **Save** in the section footer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use a product’s row menu to select **Pause** or **Activate**. Custom products also offer **Edit** and **Remove**. Custom carrier row menus offer **Edit** and **Remove**. Built-in carriers show a template badge and have no row actions. Select **Cancel** in the footer to discard unsaved carrier selections.

<Warning>Removing a custom product or carrier cannot be undone. Review the confirmation before continuing.</Warning>

## What you'll see

The product row shows **Active** or **Paused**, along with its selected carriers.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A template is unavailable">
    You can add each template once. Find the existing product and use **Activate** if it's paused.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A carrier isn't available">
    Check that the carrier exists in **Carriers**. The product's choices are limited to compatible carriers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A product can't be edited">
    Template products don't offer **Edit** or **Remove**. Use **Pause** when you no longer sell one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
