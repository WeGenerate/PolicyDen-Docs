Products and carriers drive every policy in PolicyDen. A **product** is a category of insurance your agency sells — Medicare, dental, life, and so on. A **carrier** is the company that issues the policy. Your agents pick a product and a carrier whenever they log a policy.

</Frame>

<Info>
  Only **Admins** and **Agency Owners** can add or edit products and carriers.
</Info>

## Add a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Products page">
    From agency settings, click **Products**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Product">
    The **Add Product** dialog opens with two options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a product type">
    Choose either:

    - **Medicare** — a ready-made template for Medicare Advantage, Supplements, and PDP. You can only add this once.
    - **Custom Product** — your own product type. Enter a name like *Dental* or *Term Life*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add**. PolicyDen confirms the product was created and offers a shortcut to **Manage Custom Fields** so you can design the policy form for this product.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Configure [custom fields](/settings/agency-custom-fields) right after you add a product — your agents fill them in on every policy.
</Tip>

## Enable or disable a product

Each product has a toggle on its row.

- **On** — agents can create policies against the product.
- **Off** — the product is hidden from the new-policy form, but existing policies remain untouched.

Use **Off** to retire a product without losing its history.

## Edit or delete a product

Open the actions menu (three-dot button) on any custom product:

- **Manage Carriers** — link carriers to this product (see below).
- **Edit** — rename the product.
- **Delete** — remove the product. You cannot delete system products, or products that already have policies attached.

## Manage carriers

Carriers are the insurance companies that issue the policies your agents write. Add every carrier your team uses so it appears when agents log a policy.

### Add a carrier

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Carriers tab">
    On the Products page, switch to the **Carriers** view.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Carrier">
    Enter the **Carrier name** — for example, *Regional Health Plan*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create**. The carrier appears in your list and is immediately available to agents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Enable or disable a carrier

Each carrier has an active toggle. Turn it **Off** to hide a carrier from the new-policy form while keeping its existing policies intact.

### Edit or delete a carrier

Use the actions menu on a carrier row to **Edit** its name or **Delete** it. You cannot edit or delete system-provided carriers.

## Link carriers to a product

By default, every active carrier is available on every product. If you want to restrict a product to a specific set of carriers, use **Manage Carriers** on that product.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Manage Carriers">
    From a product's actions menu, click **Manage Carriers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select carriers">
    Check the carriers this product should support.

    - Select none → every agency carrier stays available on the product.
    - Select one or more → only the selected carriers appear when agents log a policy for this product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** to apply the restriction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Restrictions apply going forward. Policies already attached to a carrier you've removed stay as they were.
</Note>
