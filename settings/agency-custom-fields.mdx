Custom fields let you extend PolicyDen beyond the built-in fields. Add the questions your agency tracks — referral source, spouse information, specific plan details — and your Agents see them on every contact and policy.

<Frame>

</Frame>

<Info>
  Only **Admins** and **Agency Owners** can configure custom fields. To learn how Agents fill in custom fields when working with contacts and policies, see [Custom fields](/customization/custom-fields).
</Info>

## Open custom fields

From agency settings, click **Custom Fields**. The page splits into three areas:

- A **sidebar** on the left with draggable field types.
- A **tab bar** across the top — one tab for **Contacts** and one tab for each product you've added.
- A **canvas** showing the sections and fields on the active tab.

Changes aren't saved until you click **Save Custom Fields** in the top-right of the tab bar.

## Field types

Drag any of these from the sidebar onto a section.

| Type | Best for |
| ---- | -------- |
| **Text** | Short free-text answers — a referral source, a plan name. |
| **Long Text** | Multi-line notes and paragraphs. |
| **Number** | Numeric values — age, premium amount. |
| **Date** | Calendar dates — a birthday, a renewal date. |
| **Single Choice** | One answer from a list — tier, category. |
| **Multiple Choice** | Any number of answers from a list — coverages, interests. |

## Add a field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a tab">
    Switch to **Contacts** or the product tab where the field should live.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag a field type in">
    Drag the field type from the left sidebar into the section you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the field">
    Click the pencil icon on the new field to open its settings. Set the label and, for choice fields, add the options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark it required if needed">
    Toggle **Required** when an agent must fill the field in before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Custom Fields** in the top-right to lock in every change on the current tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Organize sections

Sections group related fields. A contact form might have a **Basics** section, a **Household** section, and a **Compliance** section — each with its own fields.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Organize Sections">
    The button sits at the top of the canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add, rename, or reorder">
    Add new sections, rename existing ones, change the order, or remove ones you don't need. Drag the handle on a section to move it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Close the dialog, then click **Save Custom Fields** to commit your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Layout columns

Use the column selector in the top-right of the canvas to show sections in **1**, **2**, or **3** columns. Wider forms are faster to scan when you have lots of sections.

## Edit or remove a field

Hover any non-system field:

- Click the **pencil** icon to edit its label, options, or settings.
- Click the **trash** icon to remove it. The field and any data in it are deleted.

System fields (the ones shown in grey) come with PolicyDen and can't be edited or removed.

<Warning>
  Deleting a field deletes all data stored in that field across every contact or policy. Only remove fields you're sure you no longer need.
</Warning>

## Per-product policy forms

Every product you add under [Products and carriers](/settings/products-and-carriers) gets its own policy tab under custom fields. Configure **Medicare** to collect plan-specific details, **Dental** to capture coverage tiers, and so on — your Agents see the right form based on the product they pick.

<Tip>
  Set up a new product's custom fields right after you create the product, before Agents start logging policies for it.
</Tip>
