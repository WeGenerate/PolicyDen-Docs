<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners, including Admins of individual agent accounts.</Info>

Build the forms your team uses for contacts and policies. Choose the correct category before changing fields.

## Add a field

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Custom Fields">
    In **Settings**, select **Custom Fields**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/agency-settings/custom-fields.png" alt="Custom Fields editor showing field types, category selector, and Save." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a category">
    Open **Select category** and choose **Contacts** or a product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a field">
    Drag a field type from the sidebar into a section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the field">
    Open the field's edit control and enter **Field Name** and, optionally, **Field Hint**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save field settings">
    Select **Save** in the field dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the category">
    Select **Save** on the editor page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Available types are **Text**, **Long Text**, **Number**, **Date**, **Single Choice**, and **Multiple Choice**.

Use **Field Group** to choose a section. **Mandatory Field** requires a value. **Hide from Forms** removes the field from new-record forms and turns off the mandatory setting. The field remains editable on the record.

For contact fields, **Applies To** offers **All products** or **Specific products**. Text fields offer **Max Length** and **Input Mask** where supported.

Choice fields offer **Manual options** or **From a resource**. Resource choices are **Users**, **Contacts**, and **Carriers**.

## Organize sections

Select **Organize Sections** to open **Edit Sections**. Save the section changes, then save the editor page. Use field edit and delete controls only on fields you can change.

## What you'll see

Your selected category shows the updated layout; hidden fields display a **Hidden from forms** badge.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A field has no edit controls">
    Built-in and locked fields don't offer edit or delete controls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My changes aren't saved">
    The dialog saves your field settings locally. Select the page's **Save** before leaving the category.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A product category is missing">
    Add the product in **Products** first, then reopen **Custom Fields**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
