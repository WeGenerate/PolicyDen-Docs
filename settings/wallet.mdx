Your wallet holds prepaid funds for marketplace calls. Each billable call reduces the balance.

<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in accounts that use the marketplace, including individual agent accounts.</Info>

Open **Settings > Agency > Wallet**. Accounts using the marketplace see **Wallet** in place of **Billing**. Wallet does not require the dialer.

<Frame>

**Balance** is the amount available and turns red at zero or less. **Deposited** totals deposits, auto-recharges, and credits. **Charged** totals charges. **Billable Calls** counts charged calls.

## Payment methods

In **Payment Methods**, **Add Card** opens **Add Payment Method**. Enter **Card Holder**, **Billing Address**, **Card Number**, **Expiry**, and **CVC**; **Add Card** saves it.

Cards show **Default** and **Expired** badges when applicable. The card's menu offers **Make Default** for a non-default card and **Delete**, followed by confirmation.

<Warning>Auto-recharge charges your default card. Check which card is marked **Default** before turning it on.</Warning>

## Balance warnings

At $50 or less, the banner says your wallet is running low. At zero or less, it says your wallet is empty.

An empty wallet pauses priced marketplace calls. Their cards show **No funds**; free queues remain available. The banner's **Add funds** link opens Wallet.

## Who sees what

Admins and Agency Owners manage the wallet. This includes the owner of an individual agent account, who holds Admin rights. Agents inside an agency do not see this page.
