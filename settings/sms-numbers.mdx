Every outgoing SMS from PolicyDen goes through one of your agency's SMS numbers. Set up at least one number before your team starts texting clients.

## Add a number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SMS Numbers page">
    From settings, click **SMS Numbers**. You see every number connected to your agency and how many messages it has sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Number">
    The **Add New SMS Number** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick an agency">
    Choose which agency this number belongs to, or leave it as **None** to keep it unassigned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a route">
    Select a provisioning route. **Twilio** is the default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a type">
    Choose the kind of number:

    - **Toll Free** — 800 / 888 / 877 numbers, good for broad outbound messaging.
    - **Local** — a 10-digit number with a local area code.
    - **Short Code** — a 5–6 digit number for high-volume campaigns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the phone number">
    Type the digits of the number you're connecting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add**. The number appears at the top of the list, ready to use.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Active and inactive numbers

Each number has an active toggle on its row:

- **Active** — your team can send messages from this number and receive replies on it.
- **Inactive** — the number is paused. It stops sending and receiving until you reactivate it.

Use **Inactive** to temporarily stop a number without deleting it.

## Inspect usage

Click the chevron on any number to expand its row. You see:

- **Route** — the provider the number runs on.
- **Sent** — total messages sent today.
- **Delivered** — successful deliveries today.
- **Undelivered** — failed deliveries today.
- **Date Created** — when the number was added.

Use this to spot delivery issues quickly — a high undelivered count usually means the number is blocked or flagged as spam.

## Edit or delete a number

Use the actions menu (three-dot button) on any row:

- **Edit** — update the agency, route, type, or phone number.
- **Delete** — permanently remove the number. Messages that have already been sent stay on the contact's timeline.

<Warning>
  Deleting a number cannot be undone. Prefer deactivating if you only want to pause the number temporarily.
</Warning>

## Search numbers

Use the search box in the top-right to filter the list by phone number or agency name.
