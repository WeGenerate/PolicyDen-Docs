<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in agency accounts. Individual agent accounts have no Users page.</Info>

Add a user to your agency and send an email invitation. Choose the role and, for Agents, how they sell.

## Before you start

- Have the user’s name and a unique email address ready.
- For Agents, have their National Producer Number (NPN), sales channel, and employment type ready. See the [glossary](/start/glossary).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open agency users">
    In agency settings, open **Users**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/settings/users-and-roles-overview.png" alt="Agency Users page with Add User highlighted." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a user invitation">
    Select **Add User**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter user details">
    Enter **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the user role">
    Select **Agent**, **Admin**, or **Agency Owner** in **Role**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete agent details">
    For an Agent, enter **NPN** and choose **Sales Channel** and **Employment Type**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the work location">
    For **Telesales (Call center)**, choose **In-Office** or **Remote** in **Work Location**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invitation">
    Select **Add User**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Choose **Field (Face-to-face)** for in-person sales. Admins and Agency Owners manage agency settings and users; Agents use their assigned selling tools. See [role access](/start/roles).

## What you'll see

The user appears in **Users**, and PolicyDen sends an email invitation.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Add User is unavailable">
    Complete all required fields. Agent invitations also require NPN and employment details.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The email already exists">
    Use a unique email address, or find the existing user before inviting them again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I cannot find Users">
    Users is available to agency Admins and Agency Owners. Individual agent accounts have no Users page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
