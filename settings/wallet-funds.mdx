<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in accounts that use the marketplace, including individual agent accounts.</Info>

Add prepaid funds for marketplace calls, then set up auto-recharge if you want automatic top-ups.

## Before you start

- Have a valid card in **Payment Methods**.
- Check the card marked **Default** before enabling auto-recharge.

<Warning>Adding funds charges the selected card. Auto-recharge charges your default card when your balance reaches the saved threshold.</Warning>

## Add funds

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your wallet">
  Go to **Settings > Agency > Wallet**.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the deposit form">
  Select **Add Funds**.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/settings/add-funds-dialog.png" alt="Add Funds dialog showing the amount and payment card." />
  </Frame>
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a deposit amount">
  Select a preset amount or enter a value in **Custom amount**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose your card">
  In **Pay with**, select a valid card. The default valid card is selected first.
</Step>
<Step title="Submit your deposit">
  Select the **Add** button showing your amount. Complete any card authentication requested by your bank.
</Step>
</Steps>

Current presets are $50, $100, $250, and $500. Deposits must be between $25 and $5,000; follow the form's displayed limits.

## Enable auto-recharge

<Steps>
<Step title="Turn on automatic charging">
  In **Auto-Recharge**, turn on the switch beside **Automatically charge your default card.**
</Step>
<Step title="Set the balance threshold">
  Enter **Recharge Threshold**, the balance that triggers a top-up.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the recharge amount">
  Enter **Recharge Amount**, the amount added each time.
</Step>
<Step title="Save recharge settings">
  Select **Save**. **Cancel** discards unsaved changes.
</Step>
</Steps>

The current threshold range is $0–$5,000, and the recharge amount range is $25–$5,000.
{/* TODO: verify production auto-recharge limits. */}

To stop automatic charges, turn off the switch and select **Save**.

## What you'll see

A successful deposit updates **Balance** and adds a transaction; saved automatic top-ups appear in **Auto-Recharge**.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Add a card first">
  With no card saved, **Add Funds** opens **Add Payment Method**. Save a card, then reopen **Add Funds**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The Add button is disabled">
  Choose a valid amount and a non-expired card. Expired cards cannot be selected.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My payment or authentication failed">
  Follow the displayed error. See [payment fixes](/fix/wallet-and-payments) for declined cards and incomplete authentication.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Auto-recharge turned itself off">
  Repeated failures suspend auto-recharge. Update your default card, then turn it on and save again.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
