The **Dashboard** brings together sales totals, policies needing attention, and performance for the selected date range. Its sections depend on your role and account type.

## What you can do here

<CardGroup cols={2}>
<Card title="Review policies" href="/policies/overview">Open policies that need attention.</Card>
<Card title="Handle follow-up work" href="/tasks/managing-tasks">Review and complete your tasks.</Card>
<Card title="Open the live leaderboard" href="/dashboard/live-leaderboard">Compare agency sales in a separate view.</Card>
<Card title="Review reports" href="/reports/overview">Admins can explore sales in more detail.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who sees what

<Tabs>
<Tab title="Agents">
  Review **Sales**, **Active Policies**, **At Risk Policies**, and **Canceled Policies**. Your Dashboard also includes open tasks, **High Risk Sales**, and a **Leaderboard**.

  Use the date picker to change the sales reporting period. Open tasks are listed separately from that date filter. Select a policy in **High Risk Sales** to inspect it.
</Tab>
<Tab title="Admins">
  Review the sales cards, policy chart, and **High Risk Sales**. Agency accounts also have **Agents** and **Carriers** performance tabs.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/dashboard/overview-admin.png" alt="Admin Dashboard with sales cards, policy chart, and agency performance." />
  </Frame>

  Individual agent accounts show **Setup progress** while setup is incomplete. They have no team performance table or live leaderboard entry point.
</Tab>
</Tabs>

An empty section can mean there are no matching records for the selected dates. Choose a wider range before comparing totals.
