<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agency accounts. Individual agent accounts don't have this view.</Info>

Open a separate sales display from your Dashboard. Sign in to your agency account before following these steps.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open your dashboard">
  In the sidebar, select **Dashboard**.
</Step>
<Step title="Find agent performance">
  Find **Leaderboard**, or select **Agents** in the performance table.
</Step>
<Step title="Open the display">
  Select **Open Live View**. The leaderboard opens in a new browser tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose your dates">
  Use the date picker beside **Select Date Range:** to choose the reporting period.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/dashboard/live-leaderboard-display.png" alt="Dashboard performance table with the Open Live View link." />
</Frame>

## What you'll see

The display ranks agents by **Total** sales and shows **Total Sales:** for the selected range.

It starts with today's sales. **Rank** and **Agent** identify each row. Product columns appear when more than one product is returned. New sales within the selected dates can update the ranking while the page is open.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I can't find Open Live View">
  Individual agent accounts don't show the performance table. Admins should select **Agents**, rather than **Carriers**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The leaderboard is empty">
  Choose a date range with recorded sales. The live display has its own date picker.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The display failed to load">
  Refresh the leaderboard tab. If the error continues, contact support@policyden.com with the agency and selected dates.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
