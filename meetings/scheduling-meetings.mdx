Schedule a meeting when you want to set time aside with a contact — a presentation, a quarterly review, or a follow-up call. Every meeting you schedule becomes part of the contact's record.

## Prerequisites

- The contact you're meeting with needs to exist. See [Create a contact](/contacts/creating-contacts) if you haven't added them yet.
- If it's a video meeting, have the link ready to paste. If it's in-person, have the address.

## Schedule from a contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    Go to **Contacts**, open the person you're meeting with, and select the **Activities** tab on their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a meeting">
    Click the add-activity control and choose **Meeting**. The meeting details page opens for the new session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    - **Title** — double-click to name the meeting.
    - **Description** — double-click to add an agenda or notes.
    - **Meeting link or location** — paste a video link, or type an address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date and time">
    Use the **date** widget to pick a day. Use the **start** and **end** time pickers to set the window. Leave both times empty if it's an all-day event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how often it repeats">
    Open the **repeat** widget and pick **Does not repeat**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add attendees">
    Open the **attendees** widget and pick the teammates who should be there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save** in the bottom-right. The meeting is attached to the contact and appears on their activity timeline.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Schedule from a pipeline card

You can also schedule directly from the pipeline board without opening the contact first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pipeline">
    Click **Pipeline** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the activity menu on a card">
    Click the **+** icon on a contact's card, then choose **Meeting**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the meeting details">
    The meeting details page opens the same way as above — title, description, link, date, time, repeat, and attendees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## All-day meetings

Leave both the **start time** and **end time** empty to make the meeting all-day. The schedule view on the side of the meeting details page shows an all-day banner across the top of the day instead of a specific slot.

## Attachments

Click the attach button under the meeting details to upload files — a slide deck, a benefits summary, or anything you want to have handy during the meeting.

## Tips

<Tip>
  Set a clear title that your teammates will recognize ("Medicare Advantage review — John Doe") rather than a generic one ("Call"). It makes your own schedule scannable and keeps the contact's record useful later.
</Tip>

- If you need to follow up after the meeting, create a [task](/tasks/managing-tasks#create-a-task) on the same contact with the next step.
- The meeting and any recordings of the related call appear under the contact's **Activities** tab so you can review context before the next touch.
- Keep the meeting link with the meeting instead of sending it by SMS. You can always click it again from the details page.
