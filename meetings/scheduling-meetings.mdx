<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners who can open the contact.</Info>

Schedule a meeting from a contact's profile or pipeline card. Have the venue or meeting link and the appointment time ready.

## Create the meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the meeting form">
    In the contact header, select the calendar icon with the **Add Meeting** tooltip.

        From a pipeline card, select the plus icon, then **Meeting** under **Add Activity**.
<Frame caption="Add Meeting form showing the title, venue controls, date, times, and daily schedule.">
  <video className="w-full rounded-xl motion-reduce:hidden" autoPlay muted loop playsInline controls preload="metadata" poster="/images/meetings/schedule-form.png" src="/videos/meetings/schedule-form.mp4" aria-label="Add Meeting form showing the title, venue controls, date, times, and daily schedule." />
  <img className="hidden motion-reduce:block" src="/images/meetings/schedule-form.png" alt="Add Meeting form showing the title, venue controls, date, times, and daily schedule." />
</Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the meeting">
    Enter **Meeting Title**. Use **Add Description...** for an optional agenda.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the venue">
    Choose **Location** or **Meeting Link**, then enter the address or link in the displayed field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the date">
    Open the date control and select today or a future date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set both times">
    Use **Starts** and **Ends** to choose the appointment's start and end times.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a repeat setting">
    Use the repeat control to select **No repeat**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the appointment">
    Select **Create Meeting**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The daily schedule helps you compare the appointment with your other meetings. Drag its timed block to adjust the times. On mobile, select the arrow tab at the form's right edge to open the schedule.

The schedule also offers **All Day**, which clears both times. To create a meeting successfully, keep both times filled in.

## Add attendees after creation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the saved meeting">
    In the contact's **Activities** tab, select the meeting in **Meetings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your attendees">
    In **Meeting Details**, select **Attendees** and choose the agency users to add.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the attendee list">
    Select **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Add attendees here even if you selected them in **Add Meeting**; selections made during creation aren't saved.

## What you'll see

The appointment appears in the contact's **Meetings** section with its title, date, times, and owner.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The form asks for a title or venue">
    Enter a title and either a location or meeting link, then select **Create Meeting** again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The form asks for a start or end time">
    Fill in both **Starts** and **Ends**. The current create form won't save an all-day meeting.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't select a past date">
    The date picker allows today and future dates. Choose an available date.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
