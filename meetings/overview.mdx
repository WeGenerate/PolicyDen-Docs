Meetings belong to a contact. Open the contact's **Activities** tab and select a meeting in the **Meetings** section to open **Meeting Details**.

<Frame caption="Meeting Details keeps the appointment beside your daily schedule.">
  <img src="/images/meetings/overview-details.png" alt="Meeting Details showing the venue, date, time, attendees control, and daily schedule sidebar." />
</Frame>

The contact's meeting rows show the title, date, start and end times, and owner tag. In **Meeting Details**, you can review the description, comments, and **Location** or **Meeting Link**. Select a saved meeting link to open it.

The daily schedule shows your other meetings for that date. Its **All Day** control clears both times; timed meetings appear as blocks you can drag to adjust their times. Save changes with **Save**.

## Edit meeting details

Select the title or description to edit it. Use the date, **Starts**, **Ends**, and repeat controls to adjust the appointment. Repeat choices are **No repeat**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, and **Monthly**.

Select **Attendees** to choose agency users, then **Save**. Attendees selected during creation aren't retained; add them here after creating the meeting.

Select **Cancel** to discard unsaved edits. If **Save** is unavailable, check that the title and either a location or meeting link are filled in.

## Archive a meeting

Open the meeting's three-dot menu and select **Archive**. In **Archive meeting?**, select **Archive** to return to the contact's **Activities** tab.

<Warning>Archiving permanently deletes the meeting. You can't restore it from an archive.</Warning>

## Who sees what

Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners use meetings from the contacts they can open. The daily schedule shows meetings you own. The **Attendees** picker lists agency users.
