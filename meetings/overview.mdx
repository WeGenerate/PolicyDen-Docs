Meetings in PolicyDen live alongside tasks and notes on each contact's profile. You can see what's coming up, review what you discussed last time, and open any meeting for full details.

## Where meetings live

Meetings are attached to the contact you're meeting with. Open a contact's profile and use the **Activities** tab to see every meeting, task, and note in one timeline — or filter to meetings only to focus on sessions.

See [Contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile) for the full profile layout.

## Upcoming vs past meetings

The contact timeline groups meetings by date so you can tell future sessions apart from completed ones at a glance.

- **Upcoming** — meetings scheduled for today or later. Use these to prepare.
- **Past** — meetings whose end time has passed. Use these as a record of what was discussed.

Click any meeting to open its details page.

## Meeting details

The meeting details page shows everything about a single session.

| Field | What it captures |
| ----- | ---------------- |
| **Title** | What the meeting is about — for example, **Medicare presentation**. |
| **Repeat label** | Whether the meeting repeats, and how often. |
| **Description** | Notes, agenda, or context for the meeting. |
| **Location or meeting link** | An in-person address, or a video link you can click to join. |
| **Date** | The meeting date. |
| **Start and end time** | The time window. Leave both empty to mark it as all-day. |
| **Repeat** | Does not repeat, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly. |
| **Attendees** | The teammates invited. |
| **Attachments** | Files relevant to the meeting. |

A mini schedule on the right shows the meeting in the context of your day so you can spot conflicts.

<Tip>
  Click the video link to open your meeting platform in a new tab. The link is saved with the meeting so you can return to the same session later.
</Tip>

## Edit a meeting

Open the meeting details page and double-click the title or description to edit them. Use the widgets below the description to change the date, start and end time, repeat cadence, and attendees. Click **Save** to apply your changes, or **Cancel** to discard them.
