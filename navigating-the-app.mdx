## Keep the sidebar handy

Move your pointer to the left edge to reveal a collapsed sidebar. Select **Toggle Sidebar** to pin or unpin it.

For Admins and Agency Owners, **Settings** and **Dialer** open separate sidebar panels. Select the panel's back arrow to return.

Open the agency menu to reach **Profile** or **Sign Out**. Admins and Agency Owners open their name submenu to reach **Profile**.

## Who sees what

| Account or role | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| Agent | With the dialer enabled, **Call Log** and **Reporting** appear under **Main**. |
| Admin or Agency Owner | **Management** includes **Reporting** and **Settings**; **Dialer** requires the dialer to be enabled. |
| Individual agent account | You have Admin rights, but **Users** and **API Keys** are absent. |
| Marketplace agency | **Wallet** replaces **Billing** in Settings. **Marketplace** also requires Admin rights and the dialer. |
| Agency with Medicare products | Admins and Agency Owners see **Compliance**. |

Agent **Reporting** opens call reports; Admin **Reporting** opens agency reports. See [call reports](/calls/reporting) or [agency reports](/reports/overview).

For Training and Remote differences, see [I can't see a feature](/fix/cant-see-a-feature).
