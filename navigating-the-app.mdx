PolicyDen uses a single consistent layout across every page. Once you know where each control lives, you can move between contacts, policies, and conversations without thinking about it.

Every role sees the same layout. Admins and Agency Owners get extra items in the **Management** section of the sidebar and on the [Agency settings](/settings/agency-overview) tab.

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left is your primary navigation. Hover the left edge of the screen to open it, or click the pin icon to keep it open.

## The top bar

Every page has a top bar with a few common controls.

| Control | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| **Breadcrumbs** | Show where you are and let you jump back up. |
| **Search** | Jump to any contact by name, phone, or email. |
| **Add new** | Quick-create a contact, policy, task, or meeting from anywhere. |
| **Calls widget** | Place or receive calls without leaving the page. |
| **Notifications** | New messages, assignments, and mentions. |
| **Agency switcher** | Switch between agencies you belong to. |

## Lists, filters, and saved views

Contacts, policies, and tasks all share the same table UI.

- **Search** at the top filters by name, email, or phone.
- **Filters** let you narrow the list by status, stage, assigned agent, and custom fields.
- **Columns** are customizable — add or remove columns from the column picker.
- **Sort** by any column by clicking its header.
- **Saved filters** let you save the filter, sort, and columns as a named view you can reuse or share. See [Saved filters](/customization/saved-filters).

## The profile layout

Contacts and policies share a profile layout:

- **Overview** on top with identifying info and quick actions.
- **Tabs** below for related data — **Activities**, **Policies**, **Tasks**, **SMS**, **Email**, **Documents**, **Notes**, and **Comments**.

## Keyboard and click shortcuts

- **Click a contact's name** anywhere in the app to open the profile.
- **Click a phone number** to start a call from the calls widget.
- **Hover a row** to reveal inline actions like delete or assign.
- **Right-click a column header** to customize the column or change sort.

## Multi-agency users

If you work with more than one agency:

- Use the **agency switcher** in the top-left to change context.
- Data is scoped to the active agency — contacts, policies, reports, and settings only show that agency.
- Settings you change apply only to the active agency.
