<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins in an individual agent account with marketplace access and the dialer turned on.</Info>

Join a queue to receive its calls when available. You can leave later or narrow routing to eligible states.

## Before you start

- Check your [licensed states](/settings/licensed-states-and-contracts) for the queue's product.
- [Add funds](/settings/wallet-funds) before receiving priced calls.

<Warning>Each billable call is deducted from your wallet at the queue's displayed price.</Warning>

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the marketplace">
  In the sidebar, select **Marketplace**.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/marketplace/marketplace-join-queue.png" alt="Fictional Medicare queue membership with Leave queue available." />
  </Frame>
</Step>
<Step title="Review a queue">
  Check its price, hours, states, and **Licensed in** count. Use product tabs to narrow the list.
</Step>
<Step title="Join the queue">
  On your chosen card, select **Join queue**. A confirmation names the queue you joined.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Limit routing states

For a joined queue using state licensing, **Limit states** lists only states you are licensed in for its product.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open state choices">
  On the joined queue card, select **Limit states**.

  <Frame caption="Queue state choices with the Save button highlighted.">
  <img src="/images/marketplace/queue-state-choices.png" alt="Queue state choices with the Save button highlighted." />
  </Frame>
</Step>
<Step title="Choose eligible states">
  Select the state tiles you want. **Select All** selects every eligible state; **Clear** removes the selection.
</Step>
<Step title="Save your choices">
  Select **Save**. Keep at least one state selected; the selection count shows your choices.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

The card shows **Live**, or **No funds** when priced calls are paused; a restricted state selection shows a **Routing** count.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I want to stop receiving calls">
  Select **Leave queue**, then confirm **Leave Queue**. You stop receiving its calls until you join again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I can't select a state or save">
  Update your [licensed states](/settings/licensed-states-and-contracts). **Save** stays disabled without a change or with no states selected.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The queue won't let me join">
  A queue may stop accepting agents. The product must also be enabled in your account. Refresh Marketplace after correcting the issue.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The card says No funds">
  [Restore your wallet balance](/fix/wallet-and-payments). Free queues can continue receiving calls.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
