Marketplace lets you receive calls from shared queues. Each card shows the price, calling hours, and state coverage.

<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners with marketplace access and the dialer turned on.</Info>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/marketplace/marketplace-queue-cards.png" alt="Marketplace queue cards showing prices, hours, and state coverage." />
</Frame>

Cards show **Free**, a price **/ call**, or a price with a seconds threshold. Hours show **Open now**, **Closed**, or **Open 24/7**. Scheduled queues also show their hours and time zone. **Nationwide** means no listed state restriction.

<Warning>Each billable marketplace call is deducted from your wallet. Review the queue's price before joining or assigning agents.</Warning>

**Live** means you have joined or assigned agents. Check the hours too: it does not mean the queue is currently open. **No funds** means a priced queue is paused because your wallet is empty.

## Who sees what

- In an individual agent account, you hold Admin rights and use **Join queue**, **Leave queue**, and **Limit states**.
- In an agency, Admins and Agency Owners use **Manage Access** to choose agents.
- Agents inside an agency receive assigned calls through the dialer. They do not manage Marketplace or Wallet.

If no queues appear, check that the relevant [products are enabled](/settings/products-and-carriers). **No queues available yet** means no matching queues are available.
