The **Status log** tab on a policy profile is a running history of every status check recorded for that policy. Use it to see when the status changed, what it changed from, and what it changed to — without hunting through the audit log.

## Where to find it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a policy">
    From the [Policies](/policies/overview) list, click the policy number to open the side drawer, or click **Open in new window** for the full page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Status log tab">
    In the policy profile tab row, click **Status log**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The tab loads the entries for that policy. Each entry shows when the check ran, the previous status, and the new status if it changed.

## How to read an entry

Each row on the Status log is one status check.

- **Timestamp** — when the check was recorded.
- **Previous status** — the status before the check.
- **New status** — the status after, or "No changes" if nothing moved.

Entries are listed newest first, so the most recent check is at the top.

## The Status column tooltip

You don't have to open a policy to see the last time its status changed. On the [Policies](/policies/overview) list, hover the **Status** badge on any row. The tooltip shows:

- **Last checked** — the timestamp of the most recent entry.
- Whether the status changed at that check, and what it moved between.
- **Never checked**, if the policy hasn't been checked yet.

## Why it matters

Use the Status log to answer questions like:

- **When did this policy go active?** Find the entry where the new status is Active.
- **How long was it pending before it placed?** Compare the pending entry to the placed entry.
- **Did the status move unexpectedly?** Scroll the log to spot the change and when it happened.

<Tip>
  The log records status movement specifically. For a full history of every edit — field changes, reassignments, date updates — open the **Audit log** tab instead. See [Policy profile](/policies/policy-profile).
</Tip>

## Empty state

If the log reads "No status checks to show," the policy hasn't had a status check recorded yet. This is normal for brand-new policies. Entries appear as soon as the first check runs.
