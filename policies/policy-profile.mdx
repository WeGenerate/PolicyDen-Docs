Every policy has a profile where you can review the details, edit them in place, and track what's happened over the policy's life. You can open it as a side drawer from the **Policies** list or as a full page in its own tab.

## Open a policy profile

From the [Policies](/policies/overview) list:

- **Click the policy number** to open the profile in a side drawer over the list.
- In the drawer, click **Open in new window** to move the policy to a dedicated page.
- Click a contact's name to jump to the [Contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile) — the policy still appears on the contact's **Policies** tab.

The profile header shows the policy number and, when available, the contact's name and Medicare number.

## The tabs

Every policy profile has five tabs.

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | ------------- |
| **Policy** | All the policy's fields, grouped into Policy info, Policy details, and Agent info. |
| **Docs** | Files attached to the policy — applications, scope of appointment, signed forms. |
| **Comments** | Internal notes from you and your teammates. |
| **Audit log** | Every change ever made to the policy, with who made it and when. |
| **Status log** | A timeline of the policy's status changes. See [Status log](/policies/status-history). |

## Policy info

The **Policy info** card has the identifying fields.

| Field | What it captures |
| ----- | ---------------- |
| **Product** | The product the policy was written for (for example, Medicare or ACA). Set at creation and not editable afterward. |
| **Contact** | The client the policy belongs to. Click the name to open their profile. |
| **Policy type** | The specific coverage type within the product. |
| **Carrier** | The insurance company that issued the policy. |
| **Enrollment code** | The carrier's enrollment or confirmation code. |
| **Status** | Where the policy is in its lifecycle — pending, active, placed, canceled, and so on. See [Status log](/policies/status-history) for the history. |
| **Sale date** | The date the policy was sold. |

## Policy details

Depending on the product, a separate **Policy details** card may show:

- **Effective date** — when coverage started or starts.
- **Placed date** — the date the policy became eligible for full commission.
- **Plan number** — the carrier's plan identifier.
- **Advance payment** and **Placed payment** — whether the policy has been paid at the advance and placed stages (visible for agencies that track commissions this way).
- **Accreted** — whether the policy has been accreted.

## Agent info

The **Agent info** card shows the **Writing agent** — the person assigned to the policy. Admins can reassign it here or bulk-reassign across many policies at once. See [Policies bulk actions](/policies/bulk-actions).

## Medicare extras

Medicare policies have two extra sections on the right of the profile:

- **Doctors** — the client's physicians, with a toggle for in-network vs. out-of-network.
- **Medications** — the client's prescriptions, with a toggle for whether they're currently taking it.

Add, rename, or remove entries the same way you would in the contact's profile.

## Edit policy fields

Click any editable field to change it inline. The change saves when you click away, and a toast confirms success or surfaces any validation errors.

<Info>
  Some fields — policy type, carrier, enrollment code, status, effective date, placed date, plan number, and writing agent — are editable only for Admins. If you can't click a field, ask your Admin to make the change.
</Info>

## Custom fields

If your agency has set up [custom fields](/customization/custom-fields) for this product's policies, they appear in the same cards alongside the built-in fields, in the order and sections your Admin defined.

## Attach documents

Open the **Docs** tab to upload files related to this policy — signed applications, enrollment confirmations, scope-of-appointment forms, anything else worth keeping on record.

- Drag files into the drop zone or click to browse.
- Each file supports up to 10 MB.
- Supported types include documents, images, videos, audio, and archives.
- Download, rename, or archive a file from its card.

For a general overview of documents in PolicyDen, see [Documents](/customization/documents).

## Leave comments

The **Comments** tab is for internal notes visible to your team — not to the client. Type a comment, click send, and it appears at the top of the thread with your name and timestamp.

See [Notes and comments](/customization/notes-and-comments) for the difference between notes on a contact and comments on a policy.

## Review the audit log

The **Audit log** tab shows a complete history of every change made to the policy — field edits, status changes, reassignments — with the user who made the change and when it happened. Use it to answer questions like "who set the effective date?" or "when did this policy move to placed?"
