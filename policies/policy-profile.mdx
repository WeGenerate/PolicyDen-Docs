<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners with access to the policy.</Info>

Open a policy to review its coverage details, writing agent, and linked contact. Editable fields depend on your role and the product.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the policy">
    In **Policies**, use **Search** or **Filters** to find the record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the profile">
    Select the policy number.
        The profile shows policy details alongside the contact sidebar.
        <Frame caption="Policy profile showing policy details and the contact sidebar.">
  <img src="/images/policies/profile-details.png" alt="Policy profile showing policy details and the contact sidebar." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the details">
    Check the product, carrier, dates, and any custom fields shown for this policy.
    Admins can also change fields such as **Writing Agent** and **Status** when those fields appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update an editable field">
    Select the field's edit control and enter the corrected value.
    Use that field's save control to apply the change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

A successful field update appears on the policy profile; product settings determine which fields and sections are present.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A field is read-only">
    Ask an Admin to make the change. Several policy fields have edit controls only for Admins and Agency Owners.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The update failed">
    Read the **Update failed** message and check the value. Required fields and enrollment-code uniqueness still apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The Plan card is missing">
    The card appears only when the product includes its plan-number field. Ask an Admin to check the product setup.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

For contact documents, use [Documents](/customization/documents). For contact notes, use [Notes and comments](/customization/notes-and-comments).
