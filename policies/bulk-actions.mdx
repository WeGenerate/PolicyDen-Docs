Bulk actions let agency admins update many policies in a single step instead of clicking through them one at a time. You can bulk-archive policies or bulk-reassign them to a different writing agent.

<Info>
  Bulk actions on the Policies page are available to agency admins only. If you don't see the action bar after selecting rows, check with your admin.
</Info>

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Policies page">
    Click **Policies** in the sidebar. Use filters and the date range to narrow the list to the policies you want to act on — see [Policies overview](/policies/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select rows">
    Check the box on each row you want to include. To select every policy in the current view, check the box in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the bulk action bar">
    A bar appears at the bottom of the page showing how many policies are selected and the available actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an action">
    Pick **Archive** or **Assign** and confirm in the dialog that opens.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Combine filters with bulk actions for targeted cleanups — for example, filter by carrier and a date range, select all, and reassign them to a new writing agent in one step.
</Tip>

## Bulk assign a writing agent

Use **Assign** to move many policies to a different writing agent at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the policies">
    Check the policies you want to reassign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Assign">
    In the bulk action bar, click **Assign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the new agent">
    Choose an agent from the dropdown. Only active users in your agency appear here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Assign**. The writing agent updates on every selected policy, and the table refreshes to show the new assignment.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Reassigning a policy does **not** change the contact's assigned agent. Update the contact separately from [Contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile) or [Contacts bulk actions](/contacts/bulk-actions) if you want both to move together.

## Bulk archive policies

Use **Archive** to remove many policies from the main list at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the policies">
    Check the policies you want to archive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Archive">
    In the bulk action bar, click **Archive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the confirmation dialog and click **Archive policies**. The selected policies leave the active list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Archiving hides policies from the main pages for your team. The action can be reversed, but do a final review of the filter and selection before you confirm — an incorrect filter could sweep in more policies than you meant.
</Warning>

## Tips for safe bulk work

- **Filter first, then select.** Use filters and the date range to narrow the list before you check the header box.
- **Watch the count.** The bulk action bar shows how many rows you've selected — confirm it matches what you expect.
- **Start small.** If you're not sure your filter is right, run the bulk action on a handful of policies first and check the result before doing the whole list.
