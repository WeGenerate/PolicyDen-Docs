<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners with access to the policy.</Info>

Record a policy and link it to a contact. Have the client's details and enrollment information ready before opening the form.

## Before you start

- Your agency needs an active product with policy fields configured.
- Agents need a carrier mapped to their product and writing-agent record.
- Have the enrollment code and any required plan number ready.

## Choose the product and contact

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In **Policies**, select **Add Policy**.
    You can also start from **Add Policy** on a contact profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a product">
    In **Select Product**, select the product to record.
        This screen is skipped when only one product is active.
        <Frame>
      <img src="/images/policies/create-product.png" alt="Add New Policy dialog showing the Product phase." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your contact">
    In **Find a Contact**, enter the client's phone number and select **Check**.
    This screen is skipped when you start from a contact profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the contact">
    Select **Continue** for the matching contact, or **Add New Contact** when the result says **Not Found**.
    Use **Search Again** if the match is wrong.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete contact details">
    In **Contact**, complete the required fields.
        Check the contact's identity and address before continuing.
        <Frame>
      <img src="/images/policies/create-contact.png" alt="Add Policy dialog showing the Contact phase and Continue button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save contact details">
    Select **Continue**.
    The contact details are saved before you enter the policy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Record the policy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review existing policies">
    If **Existing Policies** appears, review the records before selecting **Add New Policy**.
    This screen is skipped for contacts without policies and when starting from a contact profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete policy details">
    In **Policy**, enter the required product fields, including **Carrier** and the enrollment code.
        Complete **Policy Type**, **Plan Number**, and **Writing Agent** when shown as required.
        <Frame>
      <img src="/images/policies/create-policy.png" alt="Add Policy dialog showing the Policy phase and Add Policy button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the policy">
    Select **Add Policy**.
    Wait for **Policy created** before closing the form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The enrollment field is **Enrollment Code** for Medicare and **Policy Number** for Life Insurance. Agents and individual agent accounts use their own writing-agent record automatically.

## What you'll see

The dialog closes, the policy list refreshes, and the linked contact becomes a client assigned to the writing agent.

The sale date is today. The initial status is **Unknown** for Medicare and **Submitted** for Life Insurance.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No active products found">
    Ask an Admin to activate a product in [Products and carriers](/settings/products-and-carriers).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No fields configured">
    An Admin must configure the product's policy fields in [Agency custom fields](/settings/agency-custom-fields).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Add Policy is unavailable">
    Complete required fields. Admins must choose a writing agent when that field appears; individual agent accounts use their own agent.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The form reports a validation error">
    Check highlighted fields. Enrollment codes must be unique within the agency and product. Ask an Admin to check unavailable carrier mappings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
