## Find the right policy

Use **Search**, the product selector, date range, and **Filters** to narrow the list. Select a policy number to open its profile. Selecting a contact name opens the contact instead.

## Who sees what

| Role or account | Available controls | Difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agent | Add Policy and accessible policy profiles | Some profile fields are read-only. |
| Admin or Agency Owner | Import, row selection, and profile editing | Bulk actions appear after selecting rows. |
| Individual agent account | Policy creation and admin controls | Agent and Agency list columns and filters are hidden. |

Fields vary by product and agency setup. The **Plan** card appears only for products configured with a plan-number field.
