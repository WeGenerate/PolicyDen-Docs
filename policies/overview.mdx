The **Policies** page is the master list of every policy your agency has on file. Each row is a policy tied to a contact, with a product, carrier, effective date, and current status.

  <img src="/images/policies-list.png" alt="Policies list with status preset filters, product switcher, and a table showing policy number, contact, status, product, carrier, and agent columns" />
</Frame>

## What's on a policy

A policy record captures the key details of coverage your agency has written for a client. Every policy has:

- A **contact** — the client the policy belongs to.
- A **product** — the line of business (for example, Medicare, ACA, or a product your agency has defined).
- A **carrier** — the insurance company issuing the coverage.
- An **effective date** — when coverage starts.
- A **status** — where the policy is in its lifecycle (pending, active, cancelled, and so on).

Each policy also gets a unique policy number that PolicyDen generates automatically.

## Open the Policies page

Click **Policies** in the sidebar. If your agency has more than one product, you'll see a product switcher in the breadcrumb next to "Policies" — use it to focus the list on one product at a time.

## The filter presets

Above the table, PolicyDen shows filter presets so you can jump to the policies that need attention:

- **All** — every policy.
- **Pending** — policies that haven't been confirmed yet.
- **Active**, **Issued**, or **Placed** — the in-force presets (which ones you see depend on how your agency tracks commissions).

Each preset shows the current count so you always know how much work is in each bucket.

## Search, filter, and sort

The toolbar above the table gives you the same controls on every list page in PolicyDen.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Search" icon="search">
    Type in the search box to narrow the list by policy number, contact name, Medicare number, or carrier.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Filters" icon="filter">
    Click **Filters** to combine conditions. You can filter by:

    - Agency
    - Policy number
    - Assigned agent
    - Contact
    - Carrier
    - Status
    - Policy type
    - Sale date, effective date, or placed date
    - Accreted, Fronted deal, HRA flags

    Save any filter combination as a named view with [Saved filters](/customization/saved-filters).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Sort" icon="arrow-down-up">
    Click any sortable column header to sort ascending or descending. Use the sort button in the toolbar to sort by multiple columns at once.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Date range" icon="calendar">
    The date range picker in the top-right scopes the list — and the preset counts — to a specific window. By default the range covers the current sales period.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Customize the columns

Click the columns button in the toolbar to pick which columns show in the table. You can surface fields like plan number, source of lead, SEP, agent 2, HRA, fronted deal, or any [custom field](/customization/custom-fields) defined for the selected product. Your column choices stick between sessions.

## Edit cells inline

Click a cell in most columns — policy type, carrier, enrollment code, plan number, or writing agent — to edit it without opening the policy. Your change saves as soon as you click away.

<Note>
  Some fields are editable only for admins. If a cell doesn't respond to a click, check with your agency admin.
</Note>

## Open a policy

You have two ways to open a policy from the list:

- **Click the policy number** to open a side drawer with the full policy view. This is the fastest way to review or edit a policy without losing your place.
- **Click the contact name** to jump to the contact's profile. See [Contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile).

Inside the drawer you can also click **Open in new window** to move the policy to its own page.

Full details on the policy view are in [Policy profile](/policies/policy-profile).

## Create a policy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add Policy">
    The **Add Policy** button is in the top-right of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a product">
    Select the product the policy belongs to. If your agency only uses one, PolicyDen skips this step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Pick the contact, carrier, policy type, and any other required fields. The form changes based on the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Policy**. The new policy appears at the top of the list, and its contact is automatically marked as a client.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also add a policy straight from a contact's profile. Open the contact, go to the **Policies** tab, and click **Add Policy** — the contact is pre-filled for you.
</Tip>

## Export policies

Click the export icon in the toolbar to download a CSV. You can export:

- **All** policies that match your current filters and date range, or
- **Selected** policies — check the boxes first, then export.

Pick which columns to include in the CSV before downloading.

## Import policies

Agency admins can bulk import policies from a CSV or Excel file. Click the upload icon in the toolbar, pick the product, upload your file, and map your columns. See [Products and carriers](/settings/products-and-carriers) for where products are configured.

## Archive a policy

Open a policy and use the archive action to remove it from the list. Archived policies stop appearing in the table but can be restored by your agency admin.

For bulk archiving across many policies at once, see [Policies bulk actions](/policies/bulk-actions).
