## Where fields appear

In **Contact Details**, custom fields appear within the first two sections. Section names may differ by agency.
Select a value to edit it inline. There is no separate page-wide save step.

Fields can contain text, long text, numbers, dates, or choices. The available control depends on the field's configuration.
Long text fields aren't offered as filters.

**Missing Required Information** flags incomplete required details. Fill the listed fields with the information your agency needs.
For imports, fields required for all products must be mapped. A missing value can cause a new contact's row to be skipped.

## Who sees what

Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners can use custom fields on contacts they can access.
Admins and Agency Owners configure them in [agency custom fields](/settings/agency-custom-fields).
If a field is missing or its choices look wrong, ask your agency administrator to review its setup.
