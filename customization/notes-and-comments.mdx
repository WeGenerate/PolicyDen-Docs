PolicyDen gives you two ways to write things down on a record: **notes** and **comments**. They look similar at first glance, so this page explains when to reach for each.

## Notes vs comments at a glance

| | Notes | Comments |
| --- | ----- | -------- |
| **Where they live** | On a contact | On a contact, policy, or task |
| **What they're for** | Structured write-ups you'll come back to — summaries, research, call prep | A running discussion with teammates |
| **Have a title** | Yes | No |
| **Attach files** | Yes | Yes |
| **Edit later** | Yes | Yes, on your own comments |

Use a **note** when the content is something you'd save and read again — an intake summary, a doctor list, a plan comparison. Use a **comment** when you're leaving context for a teammate or working through a decision as a team.

## Notes

The **Notes** tab sits on every contact profile. Each note has a title, a body, and any files you attached.

### Add a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the contact">
    From the contacts list, click the contact's name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Notes tab">
    Click **Notes** in the tab bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Note">
    In the bottom right, click **Add Note**. You can also click the note icon in the profile header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write and save">
    Give the note a title, write the body, and attach files with the paperclip if you need to. Click **Add Note** to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Open, edit, or share a note

- Click a note card to open it for editing.
- Use the three-dot menu on the card to **Copy Link** (a direct URL you can paste anywhere in PolicyDen) or **Archive** the note.

<Tip>
  Paste a note link into a comment or a task description to point a teammate straight at it.
</Tip>

## Comments

The **Comments** tab is on contacts, policies, and tasks. It's a chronological thread, newest first.

### Add a comment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Comments tab">
    From a contact, policy, or task profile, click the **Comments** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write in the box at the top">
    The compose box sits at the top of the tab. Type the comment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click the send button. The comment appears in the thread with your name, avatar, and a timestamp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit your comment

Open the comment's actions from the card to edit your own comments. The card marks the comment as edited and keeps track of who made the last change.

## Which one should I use?

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Intake notes after a discovery call">
    Use a **note**. Title it by call date or topic so you can find it later. Attach the SOA or consent form to the same note.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A quick follow-up for a teammate">
    Use a **comment** on the contact or policy. The teammate sees it when they open the record; the thread keeps the context with the record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Summary of a plan comparison">
    Use a **note**. It's a reference document — give it a title and save a copy of any PDFs you shared with the client.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Working through whether to cancel a policy">
    Use a **comment** on the policy. Everyone reviewing the record sees the back-and-forth in place.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
