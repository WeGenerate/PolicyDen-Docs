## Who sees what

Agents see tasks assigned to them. Admins and Agency Owners can review agency tasks and assign owners.

Individual agent accounts manage their own tasks without an agent column or owner picker. Admins and Agency Owners also see **Templates**.

Use **All Tasks**, **To Do**, **In Progress**, and **Done** to choose a view. Search by task title or filter by due date, priority, and status.
