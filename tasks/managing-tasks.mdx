Keep task progress and details current as you work. Open an existing task from **Tasks** to make changes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your task">
    In **Tasks**, search by title or select **To Do**, **In Progress**, or **Done**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the task">
    Select the task title to open **Task Details**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/tasks/manage-details.png" alt="Tasks workspace." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the status">
    Select the status control and choose **To Do**, **In Progress**, or **Done**. The change saves immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update task timing">
    Select the date control to change the due date. The change saves immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit task details">
    Select the title or description to enter your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your text">
    Select **Save** to keep title and description changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Admins and Agency Owners can also change the owner. Individual agent accounts do not show an owner picker.

To link the task to another contact, open its actions menu and select **Transfer To Contact**. Choose the contact, then select **Transfer**.

To remove a task, open its actions menu and select **Archive**. Review the confirmation before continuing.

<Warning>**Archive** permanently deletes the task. It can't be undone.</Warning>

## What you'll see

The task shows its updated details and appears in the view matching its status.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="“No tasks found”">
    Clear the search and filters, then select **All Tasks**. Agents see tasks assigned to them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Changes did not save">
    Title and description changes need **Save**. If another update fails, reopen the task to check its saved value.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Transfer is unavailable">
    Choose a contact different from the task’s current contact. The **Transfer** button stays disabled until then.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
