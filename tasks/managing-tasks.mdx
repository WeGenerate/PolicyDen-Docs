Create, edit, complete, reassign, and bulk-update tasks without leaving the Tasks page. Every action works inline on the list or from a task's detail page.

## Create a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Tasks page">
    Click **Tasks** in the sidebar, then click **Add Task** in the top-right. The **Add Task** dialog opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    - **Title** — a short description of what needs to happen.
    - **Description** — optional longer notes.
    - **Due date** — when it's due.
    - **Priority** — Low, Medium, or High.
    - **Assigned to** — pick an agent. Leave blank to assign later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach files (optional)">
    Drag files into the dialog, or click the attach button to pick from your computer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create Task**. The new task appears at the top of the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can also create a task tied to a specific contact from that contact's profile. See [Contact profile](/contacts/contact-profile).
</Tip>

## Open a task

Click the three-dot menu on the right of a row and choose **View**, or click **Task Details** from anywhere the task is linked. The task details page shows the title, description, attachments, comments, and a sidebar with status, due date, agent, priority, and creator.

## Edit a task

You can edit most task fields in two places: inline on the list, or on the task details page.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Inline on the list">
    Click a cell — **Task Name**, **Due Date**, **Priority**, **Status**, or **Assigned To** — and edit the value. Your change saves as soon as you confirm the new value.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="On the details page">
    - **Double-click the title or description** to enter edit mode.
    - Use the widgets under the description to change **due date**, **priority**, or **agent**.
    - Click **Attach** to add a file.
    - Click **Save** to commit your changes, or **Cancel** to discard them.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Complete a task

From the task details page, click **Mark As Done** in the bottom-right. The task moves to the Completed status and its title is struck through.

To reopen it, click **Reopen** in the same spot.

You can also change status inline on the list by clicking the status cell and picking a new value.

## Reassign a task

- **Inline** — click the **Assigned To** cell on the list and pick a new agent.
- **On the details page** — use the agent widget under the description.
- **In bulk** — see [Bulk actions](#bulk-actions) below.

## Add comments and attachments

The task details page includes a **Comments** section so you and teammates can discuss the work without leaving PolicyDen. Drop files under the description to attach reference material.

## Delete a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the row menu">
    On the Tasks list, click the three-dot menu on the right of the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Delete">
    Pick **Delete**. A confirmation dialog asks you to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Delete**. The task is removed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a task is permanent — it can't be undone. If you want to keep the record, change the status to **Canceled** instead.
</Warning>

## Bulk actions

Select two or more tasks with the checkboxes on the left of the list. A floating bar appears at the bottom of the screen with the available actions.

| Action | What it does |
| ------ | ------------ |
| **Complete** | Marks every selected task as Completed. |
| **Cancel** | Marks every selected task as Canceled. |
| **Assign To** | Assigns every selected task to the agent you pick. |
| **Delete** | Permanently deletes every selected task. |

Each action asks for confirmation before it runs. Selection clears once the action finishes.

<Tip>
  Select every task in the current view by clicking the header checkbox. Combine it with a filter — for example, **Assigned To = Dejan** + **Status = Open** — to reassign a teammate's open tasks in one go.
</Tip>
