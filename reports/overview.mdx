**Reports** is the Admin tool for slicing policy production. Start with a date range, pick how you want the data grouped, filter it down, and drill into the numbers. Save the combination you end up with and share it with teammates.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/reports-overview.png" alt="Reports page grouped by Agency with columns for total, pending, accepted, issued, placed, acceptance rate, effectuation, and placement rate" />
</Frame>

<Info>
  Reports are visible to Admins only. Agents see their own numbers on the [Dashboard](/dashboard/overview).
</Info>

## Open reports

Click **Reporting** in the sidebar and choose **Reports**. The page opens with the current month as the date range and **Agency** as the grouping.

## Pick a date range

The range picker sits in the top right. It controls every row and total on the page. Change it at any time — the report refreshes.

## Choose a breakdown

Breakdowns decide how rows are grouped in the table. You can stack up to three levels.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the first level">
    The first dropdown sets the top-level grouping. Options: **Agency**, **Agent**, **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **State**, **Carrier**, **SEP**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a level">
    Click **Add Level** to nest another grouping inside the first. For example, Agency → Agent shows every agency, with Agents listed under each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand a row">
    Click any row with a chevron to load its nested breakdown. The rows underneath obey the next level you picked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use Carrier → Agent to see which Agents write the most for each carrier, or Month → Agency to compare month-over-month production across your book.
</Tip>

## Columns

Columns depend on your agency's commission structure:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Heap">
    **Total**, **Pending**, **Accepted**, **Issued**, **Placed**, **Canceled**, **At Risk**, **Acceptance Rate**, **Effectuation %**, **Placement Rate**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="As earned">
    **Total**, **Pending**, **Accepted**, **Active**, **Canceled**, **At Risk**, **Acceptance Rate**, **Active Rate**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Sort by any column with the sort icon. Use the **Customize columns** button in the toolbar to hide or reorder columns.

## Filter the data

Click **Filters** in the toolbar to narrow the report. Available filters:

- **Agency**
- **Agent**
- **Carrier**
- **SEP**
- **State**
- **Status**

Click **Reset** on the right to clear every filter and return to the default grouping.

## Save a report

Saved reports keep your filters, breakdown, columns, date range, and sort so you can come back to the same view later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the view">
    Pick a breakdown, add filters, and sort the columns the way you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Type a name in the **Report name** field in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The report appears in the **Saved Reports** dropdown in the breadcrumb at the top of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To switch between saved reports, open the **Saved Reports** dropdown and pick one. To update the current report after changing something, click **Update**. Reports you don't own show as read-only — you can view them but not update or delete them.

## Share a report

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the share popover">
    With a saved report selected, click the share icon in the top right of the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick visibility">
    Choose **Visible to you only** to keep it private, or **Share with** to open the user list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select teammates">
    Search for users, select the ones who should see the report, and click **Share Report**. They'll see it in their own Saved Reports dropdown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only the person who created a report can edit, share, or delete it.
</Note>

## Delete a report

With a report you own selected, click the delete icon in the toolbar. This removes it from every user it was shared with.

## Export to CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the view">
    Apply the filters, breakdown, and columns you want to export.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the export dialog">
    Click the export icon in the toolbar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Choose **CSV** and click **Export**. The file downloads with one row per breakdown, including nested levels. Rows are prefixed with `>` for each nested level so you can see the hierarchy.
  </Step>
</Steps>
