## Who sees what

Admins and Agency Owners can open this **Reporting** page. Owners of individual agent accounts also have access, without the **Agent** filter or grouping.

Agents in dialer-enabled agencies have a different **Reporting** page at `/dialer/reports`. See [call reporting](/calls/reporting) for that page.

Product selection controls the available metrics. Medicare includes placement and persistency; other configured products can show status columns or sales totals only.

**Vendor** and spending metrics require the dialer. Spending columns also depend on the first grouping: Agency, Agent, Vendor, Month, Week, or Day.
