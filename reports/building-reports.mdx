<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners, including owners of individual agent accounts.</Info>

Use admin **Reporting** at `/reports` to answer a sales question. Agents looking for dialer activity should use [call reporting](/calls/reporting).

## Before you start

- Decide which product and sale period you want to compare.
- Check whether you need sales performance or Medicare persistency.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Reporting">
  In the sidebar's **Management** section, select **Reporting**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a product">
  Use the product selector to choose the product you want to review.
</Step>
<Step title="Set your dates">
  Use the date picker to choose the reporting period.
</Step>
<Step title="Narrow your results">
  Use the quick filters, or **Filters**, to select the records you need.
</Step>
<Step title="Group the results">
  Under **Group By**, choose a grouping. You can add levels, up to three in total.
</Step>
<Step title="Inspect policy counts">
  Select a linked count to open matching policies in a new browser tab.
</Step>
<Step title="Export the report">
  In **Performance**, select **Export Report**, then **Export** to download a CSV file.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/reports/build-report-toolbar.png" alt="Reporting toolbar with product, date, filter, and Group By controls." />

## What you'll see

The table groups matching policies, while the export includes the selected filters, grouping levels, and sorting.

Counts are linked only when drill-through is available. Rows grouped by **Vendor** do not offer policy drill-through.

The initial performance period is the current month. Initial grouping is **Agent** for agency accounts, **Day** for individual agent accounts, or **Agency** when multiple report agencies are available.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="No policies in this range">
  Widen the date range or clear restrictive filters. Confirm you selected the intended product.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A filter or metric is missing">
  Options depend on your product, account type, and dialer access. Check the [column reference](/reports/metrics-and-columns).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I can't find Export Report">
  Export is available in **Performance**. Switch back from **Persistency** before exporting.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The export doesn't download">
  Try **Export** again. If no file appears, contact support@policyden.com with the selected product and dates.
