<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners reporting on a Medicare product, including individual agent accounts.</Info>

Compare monthly retention for groups of policy sales. Start with a Medicare product that has policies recorded in the period you want to review.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Reporting">
  In **Management**, select **Reporting**.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose Medicare">
  Use the product selector to choose your Medicare product.
</Step>
<Step title="Open Persistency">
  Select **Persistency** beside **Performance**.
</Step>
<Step title="Set the sale period">
  Use the date picker to choose the sale period for your policy groups.
</Step>
<Step title="Compare monthly results">
  Review the monthly percentages and use extra **Group By** levels to compare smaller groups.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Each sale-month group shows **Sales**, **Effectuated**, **Active**, and monthly retention percentages for eligible effectuated policies.

**Month** stays locked as the first grouping. The initial sale period is the current year. A dash means no eligible policies for that monthly rate.

**Still Filling** marks the current month. **Fall Off** shading shows the share of effectuated policies lost in a month. **RDR** means **Rapid Disenrollment Rate**; the legend uses that full name.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Persistency isn't available">
  Choose a Medicare product. Other product reporting does not include this view.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="No cohorts in this range">
  Widen the sale period or clear filters. A cohort is a group of policies sold in the same month.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I can't export this view">
  CSV export is available in **Performance**. Return there to export a sales report.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
