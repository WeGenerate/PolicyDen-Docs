<Info>**Who can do this:** Agents, Admins, and Agency Owners in agencies with the dialer turned on.</Info>

Start with your status and connection in the header phone menu. Then check the causes that match what you see.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Your status is not Available">
    **Why it happens**

    - Paused, Lunch, Training, Offline, and Wrap Up prevent normal availability.

    **Fix it**

    Open the header status menu. Finish any [wrap-up](/calls/wrapping-up-calls), then select **Available**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You are taking outbound calls">
    **Why it happens**

    - Your selected direction is **Outbound**, which uses a campaign.

    **Fix it**

    For queue calls, choose **Inbound** under **Taking Calls**. For campaign calls, select an active campaign.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You are not assigned to a queue">
    **Why it happens**

    - Queue membership controls which inbound calls can reach you.

    **Fix it**

    Ask your Admin to check your assignment in [Queues](/dialer/queues). For purchased leads, check [queue access](/marketplace/joining-queues).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your queues are closed">
    **Why it happens**

    - **Queues are closed** means all your queues are outside business hours.

    **Fix it**

    Ask your Admin to review [queue hours](/dialer/queue-routing-and-hours). Return when the queue is open.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="You reached a cap">
    **Why it happens**

    - **Call cap reached** means you reached your daily call cap and were paused.

    **Fix it**

    Contact your Admin to review your queue caps and availability.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your microphone is blocked">
    **Why it happens**

    - The phone cannot connect calls without microphone permission.

    **Fix it**

    Select **Allow microphone access**. If blocked, enable microphone access using the browser’s site controls, then check **Audio Settings**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your phone is disconnected">
    **Why it happens**

    - **You were paused automatically** means incoming calls could not reach your browser. **Phone connection offline** also prevents availability.

    **Fix it**

    When you are not on a call, refresh your phone tab. Wait for **Connected**, then select **Available**. Keep that tab open.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still stuck? Email support@policyden.com. Include your agency, time and timezone, selected status and call direction, queue or campaign, and the exact error message. Include whether the phone shows **Connected** and whether other agents receive calls.
