---
title: "I can't see a feature"
description: "Find who can help when a menu item, tab, or button is missing."
keywords: ["missing menu", "permissions", "feature access", "Training", "Remote"]
---

What you see depends on your role, account type, and agency settings. Open a cause that matches the missing feature.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My role doesn't include the feature">
    **Why it happens**
    - **Management** and agency **Settings** are for Admins and Agency Owners.
    - Agent **Reporting** opens call reports; Admin **Reporting** opens agency reports.

    **Fix it**

    Ask your Admin or Agency Owner to review your [role](/settings/users-and-roles). They can change your access when appropriate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The dialer is not enabled">
    **Why it happens**
    - The agency's dialer setting controls access to dialer navigation.

    **Fix it**

    Ask your Admin or Agency Owner to check the agency's dialer access. They should contact PolicyDen support if enablement is needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Marketplace or Wallet is missing">
    **Why it happens**
    - **Marketplace** requires Admin rights, marketplace access, and the dialer enabled.
    - Marketplace agencies see **Wallet** in Settings; other agencies see **Billing**.

    **Fix it**

    Ask your Admin or Agency Owner to confirm your agency's setup with PolicyDen support. See [marketplace access](/marketplace/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Compliance is missing">
    **Why it happens**
    - **Compliance** requires Admin rights and Medicare products in the agency.

    **Fix it**

    Ask your Admin or Agency Owner to review [products and carriers](/settings/products-and-carriers) and your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Users or API Keys is missing">
    **Why it happens**
    - Individual agent accounts omit **Users** and **API Keys**, even though their owners have Admin rights.

    **Fix it**

    Contact PolicyDen support to discuss the account type your business needs. A role change alone won't add these pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An Agent can't see Live Monitor">
    **Why it happens**
    - Agent access requires **Training**, the dialer enabled, and an agency account.
    - Training access is listen-only.

    **Fix it**

    Ask your Admin or Agency Owner to review the **Training** switch on your [user profile](/settings/user-profiles).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My Contacts is missing">
    **Why it happens**
    - The **My Contacts** checkbox appears for Agents whose work location isn't **Remote**.

    **Fix it**

    Ask your Admin or Agency Owner to check your work location on your [user profile](/settings/user-profiles). They can correct it if inaccurate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still stuck? Email support@policyden.com. Include your agency, account email, role, page, and the missing feature's name.
