Match the message you see to a cause here. Check the conversation before resending a text.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The contact has opted out">
    **Why it happens**

    The inbox shows **This contact has unsubscribed from SMS messages**, or sending returns **This contact has unsubscribed and can no longer receive messages.**

    A reply containing STOP, UNSUB, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT opts the contact out. Keywords ignore capitalization and surrounding spaces.

    **Fix it**

    Respect the opt-out. If the contact independently chooses to resume texts, they can reply START, SUBSCRIBE, or UNSTOP.

  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No active texting number">
    **Why it happens**

    **No active SMS numbers available. Please contact support.** means the agency has no active texting number available.

    **Fix it**

    Contact PolicyDen support. Support provisions agency texting numbers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The phone number is invalid">
    **Why it happens**

    **Invalid contact phone number.** means the contact's phone number cannot be used for sending.

    **Fix it**

    Check and [edit the contact's phone number](/contacts/editing-contact-details), then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The message is blank or too long">
    **Why it happens**

    - **A message or media attachment is required.** appears when neither text nor a file is supplied.
    - **The message may not be greater than 500 characters.** means the text is too long.
    - **The message must be a string.** means the submitted text was not accepted.

    **Fix it**

    Enter text within 500 characters, or attach a file. Reopen the composer if ordinary text still produces an error.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An attachment is rejected">
    **Why it happens**

    - **Size limit exceeded**: **Total attachments cannot exceed 5MB.**
    - **The total size of all attachments must not exceed 5MB.**
    - **Each file must not exceed 5MB.**
    - **Too many files**: **You can attach up to 10 files.**
    - **Unsupported file type. Supported: images, video, audio, PDF, CSV, vCard.**

    **Fix it**

    Remove files until no more than 10 remain and their combined size is within 5 MB. Use a supported format.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A service or connection error appears">
    **Why it happens**

    The composer shows **Failed to send message**, sometimes followed by **Please try again.** The service can also return:

    **Failed to send message. Please try again or contact support if the issue persists.**

    Other errors can include a specific message from the texting provider.

    **Fix it**

    Check the thread before retrying so you don't send a duplicate. Check your connection, then try again. Send persistent provider errors to support.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still stuck? Email support@policyden.com. Include the exact error, time of the attempt, contact record link, and whether you attached files. Include their formats and total size; avoid sending sensitive message content.
