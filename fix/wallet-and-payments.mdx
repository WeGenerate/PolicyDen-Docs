<Info>**Who can do this:** Admins and Agency Owners in accounts that use the marketplace, including individual agent accounts.</Info>

Use the message in Wallet or Marketplace to find the matching fix.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Your wallet is running low">
  **Why it happens**

  - Your balance has reached the low-balance threshold, currently $50 or less.

  {/* TODO: verify production low-balance threshold; current configuration is $50. */}

  **Fix it**

  [Add funds or enable auto-recharge](/settings/wallet-funds) before your balance reaches zero.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your wallet is empty or a queue says No funds">
  **Why it happens**

  - A balance of zero or less pauses priced marketplace calls.
  - Free queues remain available.

  **Fix it**

  1. Follow **Add funds** to Wallet.
  2. [Complete a deposit](/settings/wallet-funds).
  3. Check **Balance**, queue hours, and agent availability before expecting new calls.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Auto-recharge failed or turned itself off">
  **Why it happens**

  - The default card is missing, expired, or could not complete the payment.
  - Repeated failed attempts turn auto-recharge off; the current limit is three failures.

  {/* TODO: verify production auto-recharge suspension limit; current configuration is three failures. */}

  **Fix it**

  1. In **Payment Methods**, add a valid card with **Add Card** if needed.
  2. Use its menu's **Make Default** action.
  3. [Turn on auto-recharge and save](/settings/wallet-funds#enable-auto-recharge).

  If calls are paused, make a manual deposit while you resolve automatic payments.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My card was declined or authentication failed">
  **Why it happens**

  - Your card provider declined the payment or bank authentication did not complete.
  - The payment form could not finish the request.

  **Fix it**

  1. Read the displayed error and check [Transactions](/settings/wallet-transactions) before retrying.
  2. Resolve the decline with your card provider, or choose another valid card in **Pay with**.
  3. Complete any authentication requested by your bank.

  For **Payment system not initialized. Please refresh the page.**, refresh Wallet before trying again.
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still stuck? Email support@policyden.com. Include your agency name, exact error, attempt time, amount, and transaction number if available. Never send full card numbers or CVC codes.
