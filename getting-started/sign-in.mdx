Sign in with the email attached to your PolicyDen account. For a new invitation, [finish account setup](/getting-started/set-up-your-account) first.

## Before you start

- Have your account email ready.
- Use a browser where you can access your email if you choose a login code.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to [app.policyden.com](https://app.policyden.com).
        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/getting-started/sign-in-options.png" alt="Sign-in form showing password, Login with code, and Login with Google options." />
        </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    In **Email**, enter your account email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    In **Password**, enter your password. You can select **Keep me logged in** on your own device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your account">
    Select **Log in**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use another sign-in method

For an email code, select **Login with code**, enter **Email**, then select **Send Login Code**. Enter the four-character code from your email. Verification starts automatically when all four characters are entered.

For Google, select **Login with Google** and choose the Google account with the same email as your PolicyDen account.

## What you'll see

PolicyDen opens your dashboard or the page you were trying to reach.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I forgot my password">
    Select **Forgot password?** and follow [sign-in recovery](/fix/sign-in-problems).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My code or Google login failed">
    See [sign-in problems](/fix/sign-in-problems) for invalid codes, missing accounts, and Google errors.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My account is not active">
    Ask your Admin or Agency Owner to review your access. Individual account owners should email support@policyden.com.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
