<Info>**Who can do this:** Owners of individual agent accounts. Agency accounts don't show this checklist.</Info>

Use **Setup progress** on **Dashboard** to prepare your products, licenses, and marketplace access. The card disappears when all five items are complete.

## Before you start

- Finish [account setup](/getting-started/set-up-your-account).
- Have your carrier, licensing, and upline information ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your dashboard">
    In the sidebar, select **Dashboard**.
        <Frame>
          <img src="/images/getting-started/setup-progress.png" alt="Individual account dashboard showing the five Setup progress cards." />
        </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your products">
    Under **Products & Carriers**, select **Start** or **Open**. Follow [products and carriers](/settings/products-and-carriers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your uplines">
    Under **Uplines**, open the item. Follow [agency relationships](/settings/relationships).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your licensing">
    Under **Licenses & Contracts**, open the item. Follow [licensed states and contracts](/settings/licensed-states-and-contracts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your wallet">
    Under **Wallet**, open the item. Follow [add wallet funds](/settings/wallet-funds).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join marketplace queues">
    Under **Marketplace**, open the item. Follow [join queues](/marketplace/joining-queues).
  </Step>
</Steps>

The first incomplete item shows **Start**. Other incomplete items show **Open**, so you can work on them in another order.

## What you'll see

The completion count increases as the account meets each setup requirement.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="An item stays incomplete">
    Products need at least one attached carrier. Uplines need an accepted or active relationship. Licensing needs both states and carrier contracts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Wallet or Marketplace stays incomplete">
    Wallet needs a default payment method and funding history. Marketplace needs a queue you have joined.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can't see Setup progress">
    It appears only for individual agent accounts with unfinished items. If every item is complete, it disappears.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
