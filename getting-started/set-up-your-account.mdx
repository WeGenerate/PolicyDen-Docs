<Accordion title="Read the video transcript">
Open the account setup link from your invitation. This example follows an invited agency Agent. In Your Profile, check your First Name and Last Name, then enter your Phone number. A profile photo is optional.

Select your Date of Birth. Use the year and month menus in the calendar, then choose the day.

Enter your National Producer Number in NPN. Check the required details, then select Continue to open Address.

Enter Street and City. Choose your State and enter your ZIP Code. Here, the address is entered manually.

Select Continue. Invited agency Agents move to Create Password. Individual accounts may show an extra Licensing step before this screen.

Enter a password that meets the displayed requirements, then enter the same value in Repeat Password. Check that all requirements are satisfied.

Select Finish to complete account setup. When the completion screen appears, select Go To Dashboard to open your account. Your dashboard is the starting point for your next tasks in PolicyDen.
</Accordion>

## Before you start

- Have your invitation email, contact details, and address ready.
- Agents need their National Producer Number (NPN); see the [glossary](/start/glossary).
- Individual account owners also need their resident state and licensed states.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Open the account setup link in your invitation email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    In **Your Profile**, enter your name, **Phone**, and **Date of Birth**.

      New agency owners also enter **Agency Name**. Invited Agents enter **NPN** when shown. A profile photo is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the address screen">
    Select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your address">
    In **Address**, complete **Street**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code**, then select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add licensing when shown">
    In **Licensing**, enter **NPN**, **Resident State**, and **Licensed States**, then select **Continue**.

      This screen appears for individual agent accounts. Select licensed states for the products you sell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your password">
    In **Create Password**, complete **Password** and **Repeat Password** using the displayed requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish account setup">
    Select **Finish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Your completion screen offers **Invite Your Team** for a new agency or **Go To Dashboard** for other accounts.
