Use your invitation link to finish your profile and create a password. The screens depend on your account type.

## Before you start

- Have your invitation email, contact details, and address ready.
- Agents need their National Producer Number (NPN); see the [glossary](/start/glossary).
- Individual account owners also need their resident state and licensed states.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Open the account setup link in your invitation email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    In **Your Profile**, enter your name, **Phone**, and **Date of Birth**.

      New agency owners also enter **Agency Name**. Invited Agents enter **NPN** when shown. A profile photo is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the address screen">
    Select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your address">
    In **Address**, complete **Street**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code**, then select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add licensing when shown">
    In **Licensing**, enter **NPN**, **Resident State**, and **Licensed States**, then select **Continue**.

      This screen appears for individual agent accounts. Select licensed states for the products you sell.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your password">
    In **Create Password**, complete **Password** and **Repeat Password** using the displayed requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish account setup">
    Select **Finish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll see

Your completion screen offers **Invite Your Team** for a new agency or **Go To Dashboard** for other accounts.

## If it didn't work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Continue is unavailable">
    Complete the required fields on the current screen. Check your phone number, address, and password requirements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My photo was rejected">
    Use a JPG, PNG, or WebP smaller than 2 MB.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The invitation or session failed">
    For a session-expired message, reload the page. For an invalid invitation, follow [sign-in recovery](/fix/sign-in-problems).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Setup returned to Your Profile">
    Recheck the displayed fields and continue. Without a saved photo, reopening setup starts at the first screen.
