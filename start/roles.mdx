## Feature and access differences

- **Dialer:** your agency must have the dialer enabled. This applies to calls and dialer management.
- **Marketplace:** your agency must use the lead marketplace. **Wallet** replaces **Billing** in Settings. Opening **Marketplace** also requires the dialer.
- **Medicare:** **Compliance** appears for agencies with Medicare products.
- **Training:** an Agent with **Training** enabled can listen through **Live Monitor**, without supervisor controls.
- **Remote:** Agents whose work location is **Remote** can view and update only contacts assigned to them.
- **Individual agent account:** **Users**, **API Keys**, and **Live Monitor** are absent. Your own profile includes **Licensed States** and **Contracts**.

**Reporting** has two destinations. Agents see [call reporting](/calls/reporting); Admins and Agency Owners see [agency reporting](/reports/overview) and can also use call reporting when the dialer is enabled.

Agency Owners can sign in as Admins or Agents in their agency. Admins can sign in as Agents only. Neither can sign in as another Agency Owner.

See [Users and roles](/settings/users-and-roles) for team setup, or [find a missing feature](/fix/cant-see-a-feature) when a control is absent.
