Set up your individual agent account, then work your first lead. As the account owner, you have Admin rights in that account. Allow about 45 minutes for Day 1.

Start with [Set up your solo account](/workflows/set-up-your-solo-account) for the complete journey.

## Day 1

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    [Set up your profile](/settings/profile) (about 5 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose products and carriers">
    [Set up products and carriers](/settings/products-and-carriers) (about 5 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your uplines">
    [Manage relationships](/settings/relationships) (about 5 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record licenses and contracts">
    [Add licensed states and contracts](/settings/licensed-states-and-contracts) (about 10 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund your wallet">
    [Add wallet funds](/settings/wallet-funds) (about 5 min). Requires marketplace access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join marketplace queues">
    [Join queues](/marketplace/joining-queues) (about 5 min). Requires marketplace access and the dialer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work your first lead">
    [Buy and work marketplace leads](/workflows/buy-and-work-marketplace-leads) (about 10 min). Requires marketplace access and the dialer.
  </Step>
</Steps>
