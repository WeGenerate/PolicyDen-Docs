Short definitions for the insurance and app terms used in these guides.

## AEP — Annual Enrollment Period

The yearly Medicare enrollment period when eligible people can change their coverage. Check the current rules before advising a client.

## OEP — Open Enrollment Period

A period when eligible people can enroll in or change coverage. The dates and allowed changes depend on the coverage type.

## SEP — Special Enrollment Period

An enrollment opportunity tied to a qualifying event or circumstance outside the usual enrollment period.

## MAPD — Medicare Advantage Prescription Drug plan

A Medicare Advantage plan that includes prescription drug coverage.

## PDP — Prescription Drug Plan

A standalone Medicare prescription drug plan.

## DNC — Do Not Call

A restriction on calling a phone number. PolicyDen’s DNC list helps your agency block calls to listed numbers.

## NPN — National Producer Number

The identifier assigned to an insurance producer through the National Insurance Producer Registry.

## Carrier

The insurance company that offers a plan.

## Product

A type of insurance your agency sells. Products organize carriers and product-specific fields in PolicyDen.

## Plan

A specific coverage offering from a carrier. You can attach plan details to a policy.

## Policy

A record of a client’s insurance coverage, including its carrier, dates, and status.

## Contact

A person whose details you keep in PolicyDen. A contact can have policies, notes, and follow-up tasks.

## Lead

A prospective client you may contact about insurance.

## Book of business

The clients and policies you service.

## Upline

An agency or organization above you in your business relationship. Manage these connections through Relationships.

## Disposition

The outcome recorded for a call, such as whether you reached the person. Dispositions help determine follow-up.

## Queue

A group that routes incoming calls to eligible agents. Marketplace queues also control which lead calls you can receive.

## Campaign

A dialer setup for making outbound calls to a group of contacts.

## Wallet

Your account’s balance for marketplace charges. Wallet activity records funds added and charges.

## Lead marketplace

The PolicyDen area where eligible accounts join queues to receive paid lead calls.

## Persistency

A measure of how much business stays active over time. Use the report’s period and status definitions when interpreting it.

## Pipeline and deal

A pipeline organizes sales stages. A deal tracks an opportunity through those stages.

## Individual agent account

An account for a solo agent, with Admin rights for setup. It has no Users or API Keys menu.

## Sales channel

How an Agent sells: Field for in-person work or Telesales for dialer work.

## Remote

An Agent work location setting. It limits contact access to contacts assigned to that Agent.

## Training

An Agent setting that allows listen-only access to Live Monitor when the agency has the dialer enabled.
