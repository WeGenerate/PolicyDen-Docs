For you if you are an Admin or Agency Owner setting up an agency and its team. Allow about 50 minutes for Day 1.

Follow [Set up your agency](/workflows/set-up-your-agency) for the complete journey.

## Day 1

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review agency settings">
    [Review agency settings and custom fields](/settings/agency-overview) (about 10 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose products and carriers">
    [Set up products and carriers](/settings/products-and-carriers) (about 5 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect agency relationships">
    [Manage relationships](/settings/relationships) (about 5 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your team">
    [Add users and choose roles](/settings/users-and-roles) (about 10 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the dialer">
    [Review dialer basics](/dialer/overview) (about 10 min). If your agency has the dialer enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up payments">
    [Review billing and wallet options](/settings/billing) (about 5 min). Marketplace agencies use **Wallet** instead of **Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send this to your agents">
    [Open the agent start guide](/start/agency-agent) (about 5 min).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Copy and send:

> Start here: [Your agency Agent guide](/start/agency-agent). Complete Day 1, then follow the steps for working in person or on the dialer.
