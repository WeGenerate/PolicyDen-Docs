For you if you are an Agent at an agency, meeting clients in person or working on the dialer. Allow about 30 minutes for Day 1.

Follow [Work a new lead](/workflows/work-a-new-lead) from first contact through follow-up.

## Day 1

<Steps>
  <Step title="Activate your account">
    [Accept your invitation](/getting-started/set-up-your-account) (about 5 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    [Review your profile](/settings/profile) (about 5 min).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your clients">
    [Open Contacts](/contacts/overview) (about 5 min). Agents whose work location is **Remote** can access only their assigned contacts.
  </Step>
</Steps>
<Tabs>
  <Tab title="In person">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Record a new lead">
        [Add a contact](/contacts/creating-contacts) (about 5 min).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Plan the next conversation">
        [Create a follow-up task](/tasks/creating-tasks) (about 5 min).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="On the dialer">
    Requires your agency to have the dialer enabled.
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Prepare to receive calls">
        [Set your call status](/calls/setting-your-status) (about 5 min).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Take your first call">
        [Answer a call](/calls/answering-calls) (about 5 min).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Record the outcome">
        [Wrap up the call](/calls/wrapping-up-calls) (about 5 min).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
